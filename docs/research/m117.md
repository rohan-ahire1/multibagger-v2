# M117: rebalance timing and seasonality on M106 (pre-registered 2026-10-04, before any run)

Asked by the user: "test out different rebalancing timing ... factor in if there is any seasonality or is there any
pattern". Dev period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31, dataset `nse_research@2026-10-02b`; before/after tax as M110.

## Part A, information: the rebalance day

M106 decides on the last session of each month (offset 0). M111/M116 showed offsets 5/10/15 score 1.08-1.41 Calmar
against 1.50 at offset 0.
1. `m117_offN`, N = 1..19 except 5/10/15 (those are `m111_ref_offN`): M106 deciding N sessions before the month's
   last session. Reported: the spread of whole-period Calmar before/after tax over N = 0..19, and whether the days
   near month-end (0-2) score above the rest (a turn-of-month effect) or the spread looks like noise.
2. `blend/m117_m106_tranche`: M106 in four tranches (offsets 0/5/10/15, a quarter of the capital each, never
   rebalanced against each other). Before tax from the blend run; after tax as the sum of the four sleeves' after-tax
   curves (each taxed alone, so no loss netting across sleeves: slightly pessimistic). Information only (the paper
   book cannot run tranches yet). It is M106's score without timing luck.

## Part B, information: seasonality

From the four offset runs (0/5/10/15, averaged so no single decision day drives it) and JUNIORBEES / NIFTYBEES:
mean monthly return by calendar month, and how many of the 14 years each month was positive. 12 months x 14 years is
too few to pick months from; this table is not used to choose a rule.

## Part C, candidate: "sell in May" (one rule, chosen from the literature, not from Part B)

Bouman & Jacobsen (2002) and Jacobsen & Zhang (2018, 114 markets including India): stock returns are lower in
May-October than November-April. `m117_halloween[_offN]`: M106, but every decision dated in April-September
(those hold May-October) is treated as risk-off by the existing filter (half the stock share to gold), whatever
JUNIORBEES does. Nothing else changes.

Pass, same bar as M114-M116: offset 0 Calmar higher before and after tax on the whole period and before tax on 2 of 3
folds; offsets 5/10/15 before-tax whole-period Calmar higher at all three; then one locked look 2024-01-01..2026-09-28,
before and after tax not lower than the M106 final run 20261002-054052-94c22192.

## Takeaways

1. Keep the month-end decision day: no other day is better on average, and moving it would just pick another
   draw. But plan on Calmar ~1.2 / ~0.95 (before / after tax), not the 1.50 from the month-end run.
2. Running M106 in four tranches gives that average without the timing luck (1.34 / 1.04). Worth building into the
   paper book if the extra orders are acceptable; that is a separate milestone.
3. Future candidates should keep being judged across offsets: a gain at offset 0 alone (as in M116) is mostly
   decision-date luck.
