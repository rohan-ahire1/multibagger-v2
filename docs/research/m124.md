# M124: a no-trade band for resizing held names (pre-registered 2026-10-23, before any M124 backtest)

Fifth cycle of the autonomous loop. After M120–M123 (four buy filters, all inside the decision-day noise band), this
cycle targets trading cost and short-term tax instead of picks.

## Idea and sources

M106 resizes every held name to its inverse-volatility goal each month. Each trim of a winner is a partial sale,
short-term taxed at 20% (M110: tax costs the book 5–5.5 points of CAGR a year; turnover ~7x a year). Never resizing
(`m110_noresize`, information) cut tax by a third but lost 3 points of pre-tax CAGR, so the resizing itself earns
something. Between the two is a no-trade band: leave a held name alone while it is close to its goal and resize it
only when it has drifted far. Optimal rebalancing under costs has exactly that form (Leland 1999 no-trade region;
Donohue & Yip 2003, *Journal of Portfolio Management*, tolerance bands), and Novy-Marx & Velikov (2016, *RFS*) show
bands/buffers cut anomaly trading costs with little loss of return. Capital-gains tax makes the case stronger
(Constantinides 1983: defer realising gains).

## Rule

At each decision, a wanted name already held whose value at the fill is within `resize_band` of its goal
(|value / goal − 1| ≤ band) is not traded; others are resized as in M106. Applies to every held name, GOLDBEES too.
New names, exits and the risk-off switch are unchanged. Engine: `Target.resize_band` in `src/mb/engine/rebalance.py`
(off when null, so every earlier run is unchanged: full test suite passes except the 3 tests that need the missing
`2026-10-02b` dataset); params `PortfolioM124`/`ParamsM124`; test `tests/test_m124_resize_band.py`.

## Candidates (M106 + one change; dev 2010-01-04..2023-12-31, `nse_research@2026-10-14`)

- `m124_band20`: resize_band 0.20; `m124_band50`: resize_band 0.50. Offsets 0/5/10/15 and 25 bps; reference `m120_ref*`.

## The bar (LOOP.md, unchanged)

Against `m120_ref*`: (1) Calmar higher before and after tax at offset 0, before tax at offsets 5/10/15; (2) no fold
lost; (3) higher at 25 bps; (4) neighbours (0.10/0.35) hold, bootstrap ≥ 85%. If both pass, the higher four-offset
mean wins. No `--final` run. After-tax numbers are reported for both (the point of the idea).

Fail: any point above. Expected: small; the pre-tax side may lose a little (less rebalancing toward low-vol names),
the after-tax side should gain. Variants tried before M124: about 998; M124 adds 2.
