# M134: promoter pledge events as a buy filter (pre-registered 2026-10-23, before any M134 backtest)

First cycle of a new loop session (sixteenth overall). A new data family; the earlier session summary asked for one.

## Idea and sources

Indian promoters pledge their shares as loan collateral. When the price falls, lenders call margin or invoke (sell)
the pledge, so the stock can spiral down. Studies:
- "The effects of promoter share pledging on stock price crash risk and financial performance: empirical evidence
  from India" (257 S&P BSE 500 firms, 2011–2020, fixed-effects panel): pledging raises future crash risk and lowers
  later performance.
- "Stock pledging and firm risk: Evidence from India" (*Financial Management* 50(1), 2021, 1,452 firms): large pledges
  raise volatility and lower firm value and long-run performance.
- "Promoter Share Pledging and Downside Risk: Evidence from Indian Listed Firms" (2009–2023): pledging firms have
  fatter left tails, and more pledging means more of it.
- NSE family firms 2009–2019 (1,492 firms): pledging is followed by lower value and higher crash risk.
None tests pledges inside a momentum book. M106's 2022 and 2020 drawdowns are partly small-cap crashes, the kind this
might screen out. Expected effect: small. About 8% of M106's buys are affected (below).

## Data (new)

`mb data fetch pledges configs/data/nse_pledges.yaml --from 2013-01 --to 2026-09` (new `src/mb/sources/nse_pledges.py`):
NSE's SAST Regulation 31 encumbrance filings (`/api/corporate-pledgedata-sast3132`, one request per month), stored
one immutable file per month in `data/raw/nse_pledges/`. 40,482 rows: creation 19,822, release 16,870, invocation
3,747; NSE returns nothing before 2014 (five rows in 2013). Each event counts from the day after its NSE broadcast,
which avoids look-ahead.
Limits, stated:
1. Before 2014 there is no data, so the rule is off and f1 (2010–13) is identical to M106. A tie on f1 counts as "not
   lost".
2. Yearly counts fall after 2019 (5,359 in 2017; 1,471 in 2023). From 2019 SEBI added depository-driven pledge
   disclosures, and some events may only appear there. The rule therefore sees fewer events late in the sample.
3. Each filing has the symbol as filed, so a later rename loses its history.

Information, with no returns (`notebooks/m134_pledges.py 20261022-194129-d756637e 180`): of M106's 836 new buys in
2015–23, 67 (8.0%) had a creation or invocation in the 180 days before the buy: 25 / 19 / 23 in 2015–17 / 2018–20 /
2021–23. Only 4 had an invocation, so invocation alone is too rare to test.

## Rule

At a decision d, a name is flagged when one or more of its pledge events (kind creation or invocation, any size) were
broadcast in the 180 calendar days before d. This is `Pledges`/`ParamsM134` in `params.py`, plus
`targets.pledge_flags`, tested in `tests/test_m134_pledges.py`.
- `m134_pl180`: a flagged name not held is not bought (`nosell`, M111's path). Held names are untouched.
- `m134_pl180h`: in addition, a flagged held name is not kept. It is sold at the decision and its slot refilled.

Each at offsets 0/5/10/15 (`_offN`) and 25 bps (`_slip25`), dev 2010-01-04..2023-12-31, `nse_research@2026-10-14`.
Reference: `m120_ref*` (M106).

## The bar (LOOP.md, unchanged)

1. Calmar higher than `m120_ref*` before and after tax at offset 0, and before tax at offsets 5/10/15.
2. No fold lost. f1 is equal by construction (see limit 1), which counts as not lost.
3. Calmar higher at 25 bps.
4. Neighbours: lookback 90 / 365 days, and creation with min_pct 0.5 (only events of half a percent of the company or
   more). None may collapse. Block bootstrap must put Calmar above M106 in ≥ 85% of resamples.

Pre-screen at all four offsets first (`notebooks/prescreen.py m120_ref m134_pl180 m134_pl180h`). Skip after-tax,
bootstrap and neighbours for a candidate that fails it. If both pass, the higher four-offset mean wins. No `--final`
run.

Fail: any point above. Variants tried before M134: about 1,012. M134 adds 2, plus 3 neighbours if one is run.
