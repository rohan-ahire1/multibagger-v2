# M28: spread the decision over four days (tranches) for the gold rotation and for the gold20 pin

Pre-registration (2026-10-01, Claude), written before any M28 run. Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only.

## Why

M27's book-or-gold rotation scored 0.960 at the month-end, but 0.578, 0.762 and 0.553 when deciding 5, 10 or 15
sessions earlier; its mean over the four timings (0.713) equals the gold20 pin's (0.709). This is rebalance timing
luck (Hoffstein, Faber & Braun, "Rebalance Timing Luck", Newfound Research 2020): a monthly all-or-nothing switch
depends on the day it is made. Their remedy is tranching: split capital into equal parts that rebalance on different
days. M11 found tranches did not help the plain book (whose timing spread is smaller); a rotation that moves the whole
book should gain more, because the four timings disagree about when to be in gold.

## Runs (question `momentum-m28-tranches`, against the pin `momentum/m19_gold20_invvol_b2`, Calmar 0.712)

Blends (`strategies/blend/configs/`), each sleeve a config already run in M19/M27, never rebalanced against each other
(as M11's `m11_tranche_b2`):

| Config | Sleeves |
|---|---|
| `m28_rot_tranche_b2` | 25% each: `m27_rot_gold_b2`, `m27_rot_gold_off5_b2`, `_off10_b2`, `_off15_b2` |
| `m28_gold20_tranche_b2` | 25% each: `m19_gold20_invvol_b2`, `m19_gold20_invvol_off5_b2`, `_off10_b2`, `_off15_b2` |
| `m28_half_tranche_b2` | 12.5% each of the eight sleeves above |

No other variants. The adoption bar is as always. `m28_gold20_tranche_b2` is also the fair reference for the rotation,
since both then carry no timing luck; the rotation is worth pursuing only if `m28_rot_tranche_b2` beats it on the
whole period and on at least 2 of 3 folds.

## Disclosure

All component runs are known (M19, M27 diagnostics tables). The blend's daily equity is the sum of the sleeves', so
its result is close to (but not the same as) a mix of known curves; drawdowns that do not coincide can make the blend's
Calmar higher than the average of the sleeves'. No blend of these curves was computed before writing this.
