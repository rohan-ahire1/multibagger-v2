# M44: no picks across a trading gap

Pre-registration (2026-10-02, Claude), committed before any M44 run. Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31; no
`--final`. Dataset `nse_research@2026-10-01c`. Base: the pin `momentum/m40_deliv40_gold20_b2`.

## Why

Momentum, volatility and liquidity windows count rows, not days. A name that stops trading in EQ/BE for months and
comes back is ranked on a window that spans years (M43: KOVAI, 2.5 years; KOHINOOR, bought 2023-02-01 across a
770-day gap, −52.8%). This is a data-correctness rule, not a return idea.

## Rule (one value, no search)

`universe.max_gap_days: 30`. At a decision date a name is not eligible if, within its last `lookback_sessions` rows
(the longest window the pin reads), two consecutive bars are more than 30 calendar days apart. Ordinary holidays leave
at most about 5 days; 30 allows short suspensions. Null keeps today's behaviour (a new params model, so existing
configs keep their model and hash).

Config: `strategies/momentum/configs/m44_gap30_deliv40_gold20_b2.yaml` = the pin plus `max_gap_days: 30`.

## What is compared

1. The standard adoption check against the pin (`mb research report`, question `momentum-m44`): whole period and every
   fold, Calmar.
2. Four-offset mean Calmar (offsets 0/5/10/15) on 2010–2023, and at 25 bps.

## Expected, and the decision rule

Known before: 7 of the pin's 2,192 entries had a window over 400 days (M43). Expected: a very small change. If it
beats the pin, it becomes the pin. If it does not, but the whole-period Calmar is within 0.02 of the pin's, it is still
recommended for the live book as a safety rule (the user decides). A larger loss means the rule removes something the
strategy relies on, and is reported as such.
