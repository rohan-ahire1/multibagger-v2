# M106: paper support for the gold switch, and a draft book for "pin + Next 50 filter"

Pre-registration (2026-10-02, Claude). Development period, dataset `nse_research@2026-10-02b`. Not an adoption test.

M105 found that the pin plus the JUNIORBEES filter (half the stock share to gold when JUNIORBEES is below its 200-day
average; keep the 25% gold; no stop) passes the bar at month-end. It needs only one thing the paper book refuses:
`regime.risk_off_symbol`. The paper book already takes its targets from the same `targets()` as the backtest, and
already holds a gold sleeve (M24), so the switch should need no new trading code.

1. `m106_gap30_g25_jr_b2` = `m105_g25_jr_b2` plus M44's gap rule (`max_gap_days: 30`), as M51 did for the pin. Check:
   within 0.02 Calmar of `m105_g25_jr_b2`.
2. Paper: accept `regime.risk_off_symbol` (the plain single-benchmark filter only; the M97/M99/M103 options stay
   refused). Tests: on a synthetic panel the paper book moves the risk-off share into the gold symbol and its
   decisions equal the replay.
3. A DRAFT paper config `configs/paper/momentum_b_top25_gold25_jr_gap.yaml`, run end to end on the daily data
   (scratch store, replay, decisions compared with the research targets), as in M51.

Nothing is deployed without the user's OK.

## Results (2026-10-02)

1. `m106_gap30_g25_jr_b2` (run 20261002-050950-93574646): 30.09% CAGR, 20.06% max DD, Calmar 1.500; folds 2.259 /
   2.200 / 1.683, vs `m105_g25_jr_b2` 1.514 (within 0.02) and the pin 1.125 (1.598 / 2.070 / 1.362). The gap rule is
   neutral here too.
2. Paper now accepts `regime.risk_off_symbol`; `tests/test_m106_paper_regime.py`: on a synthetic panel the book holds
   the 25% sleeve while risk-on and 0.25 + 0.75/2 = 62.5% gold while risk-off, and its decisions equal the replay. The
   stop, the M97/M99/M103 regime options and everything else refused before are still refused.
3. Draft `configs/paper/momentum_b_top25_gold25_jr_gap.yaml` (strategy hash 93574646…), end to end with
   `notebooks/m106_paper_e2e.py` on `nse_daily_delivery@2026-09-28.02`, Jan–Sep 2026 in a scratch store: 163
   sessions, 8 decisions, all 8 equal to the research targets on `nse_research@2026-10-02b`; replay ok, equity gap
   −0.28%, 0 mismatches. The filter was risk-off at the March 2026 month-end (gold 62.5%), risk-on otherwise.
