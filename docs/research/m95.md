# M95: the 200-day filter on Nifty Next 50 (JUNIORBEES) instead of Nifty 50

## Question

The M89-family books are mostly mid-caps, but the filter watches NIFTYBEES (the 50 largest). In fold 1 the offset-10
books' worst drawdown is January–September 2013, when mid-caps fell while Nifty 50 held up, so the filter stayed
risk-on. JUNIORBEES (Nifty Next 50, the next 50 by size, data from 2005) is the closest broad index in the data to what
the book holds. Does the filter work better on it?

| Candidate | Change |
|---|---|
| `m95_r50g_jr_b2` | `m89_g0s10_r50g_b2` with `regime.benchmark_symbol: JUNIORBEES` |
| `m95_g25gt_jr_b2` | `m93_g25s10_r50g_gt_b2` with `regime.benchmark_symbol: JUNIORBEES` |

Two variants, nothing tuned. Same checks and bar; 2007–09 only for the r50g one (the gold trend has no history then).
Disclosure: the 2013 drawdown dates were looked at first.

**Verdict.** At offset 0 both fail fold 2 (the month-end day is one of the weaker days for this filter). **In four
tranches, `blend/m95_r50g_jr_tranche_b2` passes the bar** (the report's check against the pinned run: whole period
and every fold, both baselines), **and its 25 bps copy passes too** (1.624 / 2.495 / 1.584 vs the pin's 25 bps run
1.498 / 1.971 / 1.310 and the pin's 25 bps tranches 1.494 / 2.074 / 1.260). Its 2007–09 tranche score is 0.591 vs the
pin's 0.284. g25gt_jr tranches pass at 5 bps but fail f2 at 25 bps.

Compared with M89 r50g the JUNIORBEES filter trades whole-period Calmar (1.656 vs 1.788 in tranches, DD 20.2% vs 18.1%)
for fold 1 (1.784 vs 1.377) at the same CAGR (33.4% vs 32.4%). It is the first book in this search to pass the bar in
any form.

**Caveats** before anyone adopts it: (1) the rule family (stop, no fixed gold, filter with half to gold, the Next 50
benchmark) was built over M86–M95 by looking at in-sample results, the benchmark choice after seeing the 2013
drawdown; (2) it passes only as a tranche book, not at the pinned month-end day; (3) paper code supports neither the
stop nor the risk-off gold yet. M96 checks robustness.
