## Why

M58 (top 20) and M59 (half blend: 50% pin, 50% book-or-gold rotation) each fail only fold 2. They act on different
things (concentration; crash defence), so the user's choice is likely between the pin, either one, or both. This
measures "both" under the same bar. It is a combination of two settings already seen on this data, so a pass is weak
evidence on its own; the 2007–09 window and the offsets are the real tests.
