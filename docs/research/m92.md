# M92: M89/M91 with a trailing stop

## Question

Every book's fold-1 drawdown is the same episode: a three-month slide from November 2010 to February 2011 (r50g 15.4%,
g10s10_r50g 13.9%, pin 13.1%). A slide of a few percent a month escapes a stop measured from the rebalance open. M76's
trailing stop (reference = highest close since the rebalance) cut drawdowns in such slides (tranches 16.8% vs 17.8%).

| Candidate | Change |
|---|---|
| `m92_g0t10_r50g_b2` | `m89_g0s10_r50g_b2` + `stop_trailing: true` |
| `m92_g10t10_r50g_b2` | `m91_g10s10_r50g_b2` + `stop_trailing: true` |

Two variants, both from components already tested (M76, M89, M91). Same checks and bar, tranches vs the pin's
tranches. Disclosure: the fold-1 drawdown dates were looked at before this pre-registration.
