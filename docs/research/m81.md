# M81: stopped money stays in the book

## Question

The 10% stop (M73) cuts the drawdown from 26% to 18% but costs 2.5 points of CAGR, because a stopped name's money waits
in cash until month-end (713 stops over 2010–23). Keep the stop, but put the proceeds back into the names still held,
pro rata to their value, at the same open. In a broad fall the other names hit their stops too and the book still goes
to cash; in a single-name dip the money stays in the winners (a mild tilt to the strongest names, in the spirit of
momentum itself).

| Candidate | Change from the pin |
|---|---|
| `m81_resp_b2` | 10% stop, proceeds re-spread over the held names (gold included, at its value share) |

One variant, no new number. New engine option in ParamsM81 (`exits.stop_respread`). Known before: M72–M80. Same
checks and bar as M73–M80 (main bar at offset 0; offsets 3 of 4; 25 bps; 2007–09 mean ≥ 0.284; tranches above the
pin's tranches 1.105; 1.590 / 2.199 / 1.312). If it does well, the half-blend form (as M78) follows as a separate step.
