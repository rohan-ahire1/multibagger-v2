# M20: does the gold sleeve hold up?

Pre-registration, written and committed before any run (2026-09-30, Claude working alone overnight). Development
period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`. No new strategy rule: these are checks on the M19 result.

## Why

M19's 20% gold sleeve passed, but by a hair in fold 2 (1.643 vs 1.640). Before anyone relies on it, three checks that
earlier milestones ran for inverse-vol weights (M13, M17):

## Checks

1. **Costs.** `m20_gold20_slip25_b2` = `m19_gold20_invvol_b2` on market `nse_cash_slip25` (25 bps slippage a side),
   against `m20_ctrl_slip25_b2` = `m16_invvol_b2` on the same market (question `momentum-m20-slip25`). The sleeve
   passes if it beats the control on Calmar for the whole period and folds 1 and 3; fold 2 is reported (M19 showed it
   is a coin flip there).
2. **Bootstrap.** `notebooks/m20_bootstrap.py`: the M17 method (paired stationary block bootstrap, mean block 63
   sessions, 5,000 resamples, seed 20260930) on the daily returns of gold20 (run 20260929-202720-5f07ff7a), the pin
   (20260929-195717-eb7a8fe9), gold30 (20260929-202729-cdf2da26), 12-1 momentum and the Nifty 500 TRI. Reported: the share
   of resamples in which gold20 has the higher Calmar than each of the others, and the 5–95% range of the difference.
   Read as: the sleeve's edge is solid if gold20 beats the pin in at least 80% of resamples.
3. **Capacity.** `notebooks/m20_capacity.py`: the M13 measure (fill value as a share of that day's turnover, at a
   ₹10 lakh book) for GOLDBEES fills alone and for all fills; plus the book size at which the p95 GOLDBEES fill is 5%
   of its day's turnover.

## Disclosure

Known before: all M19 results. The bootstrap of two books that share 80% of their holdings will favour whichever has
the higher Calmar on the actual path unless the gold part adds real noise; the pin-vs-gold20 correlation will be
reported beside it.

## Results (2026-09-30)

1. **Costs: passes.** At 25 bps slippage a side (question `momentum-m20-slip25`):

   | Config | Run | CAGR | Max DD | Calmar | f1 / f2 / f3 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | `m20_gold20_slip25_b2` | 20260929-203134-28967c8e | 21.52% | 32.11% | 0.670 | 0.438 / 1.558 / 0.780 |
   | `m20_ctrl_slip25_b2` | 20260929-203143-20f11b19 | 23.99% | 38.46% | 0.624 | 0.315 / 1.528 / 0.711 |

   The sleeve wins the whole period and all three folds, fold 2 included. Costs cut both books' Calmar by about 0.04.

2. **Bootstrap: passes (91.9% ≥ 80%).** `notebooks/m20_bootstrap.py`, 5,000 resamples:

   | Gold20 vs | Daily corr | Actual Calmar diff | Gold20 higher Calmar | Calmar diff 5–95% | Gold20 higher CAGR |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | pin `m16_invvol_b2` | 0.985 | +0.045 | 91.9% | −0.013 to +0.182 | 7.9% |
   | gold30 | 0.993 | −0.027 | 13.3% | −0.098 to +0.022 | 94.3% |
   | 12-1 momentum | 0.947 | +0.239 | 99.8% | +0.109 to +0.513 | 53.9% |
   | Nifty 500 TRI | 0.793 | +0.383 | 99.2% | +0.153 to +0.703 | 100.0% |

   Calmar 5–50–95%: gold20 0.378 / 0.730 / 1.401; pin 0.301 / 0.670 / 1.324; gold30 0.419 / 0.764 / 1.425.
   The trade-off is plain: gold lowers CAGR in 92% of resamples and raises Calmar in 92%. Gold30 would raise Calmar
   more (in 87% of resamples) at a further ~1.5 points of CAGR; it failed M19's fold-2 bar and stays unadopted.

3. **Capacity: not a limit.** `notebooks/m20_capacity.py`, at a ₹10 lakh book: GOLDBEES's 175 fills have median
   0.008% and p95 0.060% of the day's turnover (p95 reaches 5% at a ₹8.3 crore book). All 9,268 fills: p95 0.092%
   (5% at ₹5.4 crore), the same limit as the stock book alone (M13: ~₹4–5 crore). Ten fills have no turnover bar;
   one fill reached 26.8% of a thin day, as in M13.

Conclusion: the 20% gold sleeve survives higher costs, resampling and capacity. It stays the research reference;
paper use still needs the user's decision.
