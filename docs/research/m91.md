# M91: M89 r50g with a fixed gold slice

## Question

M89's r50g book fails only fold 1 (2010–13), on drawdown (15.4% vs the pin's 13.1%), while earning more. The pin's
fixed 25% gold is what kept its 2011 drawdown small. Does a fixed gold slice on top of the r50g rule fix fold 1, and
at what cost in return?

| Candidate | Change from `m89_g0s10_r50g_b2` |
|---|---|
| `m91_g10s10_r50g_b2` | `fixed_weights: {GOLDBEES: 0.10}` (M74's 0.10) |
| `m91_g25s10_r50g_b2` | `fixed_weights: {GOLDBEES: 0.25}` (the pin's 0.25) |

When risk-off, the gross 0.5 applies to the whole book (stocks and fixed gold) and the freed half buys gold, so gold is
0.55 / 0.625 of the book. Both weights come from earlier milestones, not tuned here. Two variants; same checks and bar
as M73–M89, tranches vs the pin's tranches. Expected: f1 closer, CAGR 1–3 points lower.
