# M54: the new pin in four tranches (decision offsets 0, 5, 10, 15)

Pre-registered 2026-10-02, before any run.

## Idea

The pin `m49_b_b2` (Calmar 1.125, run 20261002-000607-0103cc7f) decides on one day a month. Its offset runs differ
(1.101 / 1.055 / 1.142 at offsets 5 / 10 / 15), which is timing luck. Splitting the capital into four sleeves that each
decide on a different offset averages that luck away (Hoffstein, Faber & Braun 2020, "Rebalance timing luck"). M28
found tranches cut drawdown on the old gold20 pin at a small CAGR cost; this checks the same for the new pin.

One variant only. The adoption bar is as always (whole period and every fold vs the pin and both baselines). If it
passes: 25 bps check (four `m49_b_slip25`-style sleeves) and capacity (each sleeve trades a quarter of the book).
