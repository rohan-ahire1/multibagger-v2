# M22: hold the gold sleeve only while gold is trending up

Pre-registration, written and committed before the code is used for any run (2026-09-30, Claude working alone overnight
at the user's request for a higher Calmar). Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`.

## Why

M19's gold sleeve cut drawdowns but gave up return in fold 2 (2014–17), when gold went nowhere and Indian momentum did
very well; that is why the 30% sleeve failed. Time-series momentum (Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012) is one of the best
documented effects in commodities: an asset that rose over the last year tends to keep rising. Holding gold only while
its own 12-month return is positive should keep the sleeve in the years it helps and hand its weight to the stock picks
in the years it drags.

## The rule (`PortfolioM22.fixed_trend_sessions`, a new config model `ParamsM22`)

At each decision date, a fixed symbol is held at its weight only if its adjusted close is above its close
`fixed_trend_sessions` rows (sessions) earlier. Otherwise it is sold and the picks take the whole book, exactly as in
`m16_invvol_b2`. No other change.

## Runs (question `momentum-m22-gold-trend`, against the pin `momentum/m19_gold20_invvol_b2`, Calmar 0.712)

| Config | Is |
|---|---|
| `m22_gold20_trend252_b2` | `m19_gold20_invvol_b2` + `fixed_trend_sessions: 252` |
| `m22_gold30_trend252_b2` | the same with `fixed_weights: {GOLDBEES: 0.30}` |

252 sessions (one year, no skip) is the paper's standard lookback, chosen now; no other lookback will be tried. Adoption
as always: beat the pin and both baselines on Calmar, whole period and every fold. If one passes: the M15 offsets
(5, 10, 15) against the gold20 offset runs from M19, adopted only if it wins at most of them; then 25 bps slippage
against `m20_gold20_slip25_b2`; then the M17/M20 bootstrap against the pin.

## Disclosure

- Known before: all M19–M21 results, and (from a look at GOLDBEES's data made while writing this) the months the rule
  would be off: 9 in 2013, 9 in 2014, 12 in 2015, 1 in 2016, 8 in 2017, 8 in 2021, 1 in 2022, none in 2010–12,
  2018–20 or 2023. So it is off for much of fold 2 and on through the 2020 crash. This makes a pass likely by
  construction; the offsets and costs checks carry more weight than usual, and the result should be read as "the
  sleeve's cost in fold 2 can be avoided by a standard rule", not as independent evidence.
- A trend rule on one asset can whipsaw: 2016, 2021 and 2022 are the risk.
