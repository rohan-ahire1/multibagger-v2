# M65: market impact at larger book sizes (information)

Pre-registered 2026-10-02, before running `notebooks/m65_impact.py`.

## Why

The capacity check (M40, M49) only measures the share of a day's turnover a fill takes at ₹10 lakh. The candidates
differ in how concentrated they are (top 25, 20, 15; half the book in a rotation sleeve), so their edge may not survive
the same book size equally. This charges every stock fill a square-root impact, Y·σ·√(fill / day's turnover) of its
value, with Y = 1 and σ the stock's 21-session daily volatility (Tóth et al. 2011, "Anomalous price impact and the
critical nature of liquidity in financial markets"; Frazzini, Israel & Moskowitz 2018, "Trading Costs"), on top of
the market config's costs. Book sizes ₹10 lakh, ₹50 lakh, ₹2 crore and ₹10 crore are held constant through 2010–23,
so early, thinner years are charged more. Y = 1 is the round textbook value, not fitted.

Books: pin `m49_b_b2`, top 20 `m58_top20_b2`, top 15 `m64_top15_b2`, half `m59_half_b2`, M61 `m61_half_top20_b2`.
Nothing is adopted from M65; it tells the user how large each book can be run.
