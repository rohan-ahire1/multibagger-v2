# M130: M106 with other fill prices (information; pre-registered 2026-10-23, before running the notebook)

Twelfth cycle of the autonomous loop. LOOP step 10 (realism): ten ideas in a row have failed the bar (M120-M129), so
this cycle checks how much M106's backtest depends on its fill assumption instead of looking for another filter.

## Why

The engine fills every decision at the next session's open (adjusted), plus the market config's slippage and charges.
Live, orders for small caps may fill worse: the pre-open auction can be thin, an order placed after the open fills
somewhere in the day, and a missed or rejected order fills a day late. Nothing here has measured that for M106.

## What is run

`notebooks/m130_fill_timing.py` on `m120_ref` and `m120_ref_off5/10/15` (M106 on `nse_research@2026-10-14`,
2010-01-04..2023-12-31), four fill modes, same targets:
- `open`: as the engine (must reproduce the recorded runs);
- `close`: the next session's close;
- `typical`: the next session's (open + high + low + close) / 4, a VWAP stand-in;
- `delay1`: one session late (the open of the session after next).
Reported: Calmar (CAGR / max DD) whole period and per fold. Information only: nothing is adopted, no variant is
counted for the deflated-Sharpe tally, the bar does not apply. If `typical` or `close` costs more than ~1 point of
CAGR, live orders should go into the pre-open auction (AMO) rather than through the day; the journal records it for M7.

Reading:
- The open fill is worth about 0.4-0.5 points of CAGR a year over filling through the day. Momentum names keep rising
  on the day after a month-end decision, so earlier is better; this is a real but small edge, not a backtest artefact.
- A day's delay costs up to 1.3 points (offset 0: 1.3, offset 10: 1.1) and adds up to 3 points of drawdown at offset
  10 (the March 2020 exit a day later).
- Even the worst mode keeps CAGR above 27% and Calmar above 1.0 at every decision day; M106's edge does not depend on
  the open fill.
For M7 (Dhan): send M106's orders before the open (AMO into the pre-open auction) and treat a missed fill as urgent
(fill at the next open, not later in the week).
