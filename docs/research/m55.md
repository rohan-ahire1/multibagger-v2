# M55: rank on the daytime part of the return (open to close) instead of the whole return

Pre-registered 2026-10-02, before any code or run.

## Idea

A stock's daily return splits into overnight (yesterday's close to today's open) and daytime (open to close). Lou,
Polk & Skouras (2019, JFE, "A tug of war") show the two parts come from different investors and behave differently.
Barardehi, Bogousslavsky & Muravyev ("What drives momentum and reversal? Evidence from day and night signals") find
that the momentum effect is carried by past daytime returns, where institutions trade, while past overnight returns,
where retail orders at the open dominate, add noise or reverse. Indian small caps have heavy retail opening
orders, so the split may matter here too. The delivery filter (M40) already removes speculative volume; this is a
second, independent way to remove retail noise from the signal.

## Change

`ranking.return_part`: `total` (as now), `intraday` or `overnight`. With `intraday`, momentum = exp(sum of
log(adj_close / adj_open) over the same rows as now: the `lookback − skip` sessions ending `skip` sessions before the
decision) − 1; `overnight` uses log(adj_open / previous adj_close). The vol in `return_over_vol` stays the stdev of
whole daily returns. A name with a missing or non-positive open in the window is not eligible.
