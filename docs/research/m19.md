# M19: a fixed gold sleeve inside the momentum book

Pre-registration, written and committed before the code is used for any run (2026-09-30, Claude, at the user's
request for a higher Calmar). Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`.

## Why

Calmar is CAGR / max drawdown. Holding part of the book in cash lowers both by about the same share, so it cannot
raise Calmar much. An asset that does not fall with Indian stocks can: it adds its own return and softens the
drawdown. Gold is the obvious one for an Indian investor, and `GOLDBEES` (Nippon India gold ETF, listed 2007) covers
the whole period. The drawdown controls already tried (market regime to cash, market-volatility target, stop-loss,
sector cap, absolute momentum) all acted on the stocks themselves.

## The rule (a new config model, `PortfolioM19`)

`fixed_weights: {symbol: weight}`. At every decision date the book targets each fixed symbol at its weight of equity;
the momentum picks share the rest with their usual relative weights (inverse-vol here). The simulator trades the
difference at the next open with the market's normal costs, so the month-end top-up back to the fixed weight is
charged like any other trade. The fixed symbol is not ranked and is not subject to the universe filters. Existing
configs keep their model and hash.

## Runs (question `momentum-m19-gold`, against the pin `momentum/m16_invvol_b2`, Calmar 0.667)

| Config | Is |
|---|---|
| `m19_gold20_invvol_b2` | `m16_invvol_b2` + `fixed_weights: {GOLDBEES: 0.2}` |
| `m19_gold30_invvol_b2` | `m16_invvol_b2` + `fixed_weights: {GOLDBEES: 0.3}` |

Two variants only, weights chosen now as round numbers. Adoption as always: beat the pin and both baselines on Calmar,
whole period and every fold. If one passes, it is also run at the M15 decision offsets (0..7 sessions before the
month-end) against `m16_invvol_b2` at the same offsets, and it is adopted only if it wins at most of them.

Also reported: CAGR and max DD against the pin, the daily return correlation between gold and the stock book, and
where the book's worst drawdown moves.

## Disclosure

- Known before this: the pin's results, and that gold rose about 3.2× over 2010–2023 in rupees with a flat 2013–2018

  ₹8.2 crore a day).
- Expected weak spot: fold 2 (2014–17), when gold was flat and momentum did very well.
- Paper trading is stocks only; a passing result would need the user's decision on holding an ETF in a book.
