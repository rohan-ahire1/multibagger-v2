# M59: book-or-gold rotation on the new pin

Pre-registered 2026-10-02, before any M59 run.

## Why

The pin `m49_b_b2` lost 51% in 2007–09 (M48: `m48_early_b`, run 20261002-000204-b9a5eea5, Calmar 0.26). A 2008-type
bear market is the real risk to a live book, and 2010–23 has none. The only structure here with a known defence is
the M27 rotation (Faber 2007; Antonacci 2014; Keller): each month the book and GOLDBEES are scored on 1, 3, 6 and 12-month
returns, and the winner of each lookback gets a quarter of the capital. On older books it cut the 2007–09 loss from
65% to 38% but whipsawed in 2010–17 (M27, M36). The new book is stronger and has a smaller drawdown, so the
trade-off may differ.
