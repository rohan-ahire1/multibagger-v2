# M94: robustness of M93's g25s10_r50g_gt tranches (information)

## Question

M93's g25s10_r50g_gt in four tranches beat the pinned run and the pin's tranches everywhere at 5 bps, failing only the
25 bps check on fold 1. Before anyone weighs it, how robust is it? Same three checks as M90:

1. **Neighbours:** trend lookback 189 and 378 sessions (M22 had fixed 252; these are the obvious ±), gold 0.20 and
   0.30 (±0.05). Tranches for each. Information only.
2. **Bootstrap** (M20 settings, 5,000 stationary-bootstrap resamples, 63-day blocks): g25gt tranches vs the pin's
   tranches, vs M89 r50g tranches, vs M78 half + stop tranches.
3. **Stitched walk-forward 2014–23** (M52): pieces on M42's A / B / C settings with the g25gt rule.

Nothing is adopted from this milestone.

Whole period, f2 and f3 are far above the pin in every neighbour (1.68–1.94). **Fold 1 is a coin flip**: two of four
neighbours fall below the pin's tranches (g20 1.568, 378 sessions 1.540). Every neighbour's offset-10 sleeve has fold 1
near 0.8–0.96: that decision day hits the 2011 slide badly in every M89-family book.

**2. Bootstrap** (`uv run python notebooks/m94_bootstrap.py`): g25gt tranches beat the pin's tranches in 86.5% of
resamples (Calmar diff 5–95% −0.24 to +1.00) but have higher CAGR in only 27.6%; vs r50g tranches, 59.7% (CAGR higher
in 1.0%). Median Calmar g25gt 1.65, r50g 1.61, pin 1.16.

| g25gt | 29.85%, 19.68%, 1.517 | 29.66%, 16.73%, 1.773 | 2.102 / 1.254 / 2.548 |

**Reading.** g25gt is r50g with insurance: about 4 points less CAGR for 1–2 points less drawdown. Bootstrap says the two
are the same on Calmar (60/40) and r50g almost surely earns more. g25gt's only advantage is the bar's fold 1, and that
edge is fragile (two of four neighbours lose it). If the user wants the M89 family, r50g is the higher-return choice and
g25gt the one closer to passing the formal bar; neither is a clean pass.
