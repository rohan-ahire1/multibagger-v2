# M88: the no-gold stop book with a 200-day market filter

## Question

M86: with no gold, the 10% stop book makes 32.8% CAGR in tranches (Calmar 1.509) but loses 55% in 2008, because a
stop that resets every month does not stop a slow bear. Gold covered slow bears but costs ~3 points of CAGR in bull
years. The classic slow-bear tool is a trend filter on the index (Faber 2007): hold the stocks only while the market
is above its 200-day average. M5 found it failed on the old book without a stop; here the stop handles fast crashes and
the filter only needs to handle slow ones. The option already exists (`regime`, M5).

| Candidate | Change from `m86_g0s10_b2` |
|---|---|
| `m88_g0s10_r0_b2` | `regime: {enabled: true, benchmark_symbol: NIFTYBEES, sma_sessions: 200, risk_off_gross: 0.0}` |
| `m88_g0s10_r50_b2` | same with `risk_off_gross: 0.5` |

The filter is read at each decision date only (monthly). 200 sessions and NIFTYBEES are M5's settings, not tuned; 0
and 0.5 are the two natural choices (all out, half out). Same checks and bar as M73–M86. Expected: lower 2008 loss;
risk of whipsaw costs in 2011, 2015–16, 2018–19.
