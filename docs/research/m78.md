# M78: the 10% stop inside the half rotation blend

## Question

Two defences that work on different crashes: the 10% stop (M73) cut the fast ones (March 2020: 26.7% → 17.8%) but
not 2008 (2007–09 0.415); the half rotation blend (M59, 50% pin + 50% book-or-gold rotation) helped most in the slow
2008 bear (2007–09 tranches 0.617) but little in 2020. Combine them: put the same 10% stop in both sleeves.

| Candidate | Sleeves (fixed 50/50, never rebalanced) |
|---|---|
| `m78_half_s10_b2` | pin + 10% stop (`m72_stop10_b2`) and rotation + 10% stop (`m78_rot_s10_b2`) |
| `m78_half_g10s10_b2` | gold 0.10 + 10% stop (`m74_g10s10_b2`) and rotation + 10% stop |

Two variants; the rotation sleeve is `m59_rot_b2` with `stop_loss_frac: 0.1`. Known before: each part on its own (M59,
M73, M74, M68 tranches); this combination was not computed. Same checks and bar as M73–M77: the pin and baselines on
the whole period and each fold; offsets 3 of 4; 25 bps; 2007–09 mean ≥ 0.284; tranches (eight 12.5% sleeves) above the
pin's tranches (1.105; 1.590 / 2.199 / 1.312).

Information: these are the best all-round books found. In tranches, half_s10 beats the pin's tranches in f1 and f3
and loses only f2 (2.080 vs 2.199); half_g10s10 beats them in f2 and f3 and loses f1 (1.515 vs 1.590). Neither
beats them in all three. Rotation with a stop on its own scores ~1.0–1.13 on 2007–09.

Bootstrap (`notebooks/m78_bootstrap.py`, M20 settings): half_s10 tranches vs pin tranches — Calmar higher in 83.2% of
resamples, CAGR lower in 97.1% (−4.6 to −0.4 points); vs half_g10s10 tranches, Calmar higher in 64.6%, CAGR lower in
99.5%. half_g10s10 vs pin tranches: Calmar higher in 82.1%.

The cost is in strong years: 2014 +53% (half_g10s10 +61%), 2021 +108% (+122%).
