# M69: the tranche books at 25 bps (information)

Pre-registered 2026-10-02, before any M69 run.

## Why

M68 found the half blend in decision-day tranches beating the pinned run everywhere. M49's bar also asks for the
25 bps check, which exists only at offset 0. This makes 25 bps copies of the offset 5/10/15 sleeves (market
`nse_cash_slip25`, nothing else changed) and runs three tranche books at 25 bps: the pin's (`m69_pin_tranche_slip25_b2`),
the half blend's (`m69_half_tranche_slip25_b2`) and M61's (`m69_m61_tranche_slip25_b2`). Nothing is adopted;
the numbers go to the user with M68.

New sleeves: `m69_b_off{5,10,15}_slip25_b2` (from `m49_b_off*_b2`), `m69_rot_off{5,10,15}_slip25_b2` (from
`m59_rot_off*_b2`), `m69_top20_off{5,10,15}_slip25_b2` (from `m58_top20_off*_b2`), `m69_rot_top20_off{5,10,15}_slip25_b2`
(from `m61_rot_top20_off*_b2`).
