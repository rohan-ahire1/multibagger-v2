# M129: momentum acceleration in the rank (pre-registered 2026-10-23, before any M129 backtest)

Eleventh cycle of the autonomous loop.

## Idea and sources

Two stocks with the same 12-month return can have got there differently: one rising faster lately (convex log-price
path, accelerating), one that rose early and flattened (concave). Acceleration, the change in momentum, predicts
returns beyond momentum: Ardila, Forró & Sornette (2015, Swiss Finance Institute RP 15-30, "The acceleration effect
and Gamma factor in asset pricing": a Γ factor from acceleration explains momentum-sorted portfolios, not the reverse);
Chen & Yu (momentum acceleration, US 1962-2014: quadratic-fit coefficient 0.95% / month vs 0.63% for momentum, CXO
summary). Gettleman & Marks (2006) earlier "acceleration strategies". All US; no Indian test found. In this project the
recent part of the window carries the signal (M45: months 12-7 alone fail), which fits. Not tried here (grep: no
acceleration / convexity rule). M128 (downside score) failed; this cycle changes the ranking again, and the four-offset
pre-screen guards against the M127-style month-end luck.

Dropped this cycle before pre-registration (not counted): (a) risk-off concentration (no evidence; a Taiwan fund
study points the other way, diversify in bears); (b) aggregate-illiquidity state (Avramov, Cheng & Hameed 2016 JFQA):
India's high-illiquidity months are 2013-08, early 2016, 2016-11, 2018-10, 2019, 2020-03/04, i.e. bottoms before
rebounds, the same failure as M122; (c) the JUNIORBEES switch checked weekly: 56 flips vs 26 in 2010-23, the extra ones
whipsaws, and the 2020 and 2022 breaks are caught at the same month-ends.

## Rule

Acceleration = c from a least-squares fit log adj_close = a + b·t + c·t² over the same 259 rows the score uses (ending
14 sessions before the decision). Among eligible names (after M106's filters), order key = 0.5 × percentile rank of
M106's score + 0.5 × percentile rank of c; top 25 and the hold rule use that order. Everything else is M106. Code:
`RankingM129.accel_rank_weight`, `accelerations` and `ranked` in `targets.py`; `tests/test_m129_acceleration.py`.
