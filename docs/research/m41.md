# M41: walk-forward parameter search with Optuna (pilot)

Pre-registration (2026-10-02, Claude), written and committed before any search run. Development period
2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`. Dataset `nse_research@2026-10-01c`. Base config: the pin
`momentum/m40_deliv40_gold20_b2` (Calmar 0.913, folds 1.10/1.98/1.01).

## Why

The user asked whether an optimiser (Optuna) can find better settings. Tuning seven settings on 2010–2023 and then
quoting the 2010–2023 score would only measure how well the search fits that history. So the search is walk-forward:
settings are tuned on the past and scored on the years that follow, which the search never saw. This pilot is small.
Its job is to measure (a) the gap between tuned and next-period scores and (b) whether the search space is sensible,
before a larger run. **Nothing from the pilot is adopted.**

## Objective and method

- One trial = one setting. Its score is the **mean Calmar over decision offsets 0, 5, 10 and 15** on the training
  window, from a run that starts 2010-01-04 with the panel cut at the training end. Averaging the offsets keeps
  rebalance-day luck out of the score (M37).
- A trial whose holdings touch an unexplained price jump (the data gate) scores nothing and is recorded as failed.
- Sampler: Optuna TPE, seed 41. The pin's settings are queued as the first trial so its training score is known.
- **Step A:** train 2010-01-04..2013-12-31 (f1), 60 trials; test on f2 (2014–2017).
- **Step B:** train 2010-01-04..2017-12-31 (f1+f2), 60 trials; test on f3 (2018–2023).
- Selected setting per step: the trial with the best training score. Its test score is the mean Calmar over the four
  offsets on the test fold, sliced from a 2010-01-04..2023-12-31 run (how fold numbers are always computed).
- The top 5 trials per step are also scored on the test fold, as information on how stable the ranking is.

## What is compared

For each step's test fold, the four-offset mean Calmar of: the selected setting, the top 5, and the pin. The pin is
**not** a clean out-of-sample benchmark: its settings were chosen over M5–M40 by looking at all of 2010–2023. The
walk-forward number is the honest one; the gap between the two says how much of the pin's 0.913 to trust.

## Amendment (2026-10-02, before any result other than the pin's own trial)

The first launch crashed on trial 3: a 210-session volatility window has no data for names listed less than 210
sessions earlier, which a 126-session lookback admits. The volatility window is now capped at the lookback. The only
completed trial was the queued pin (training Calmar 0.865, its known f1 behaviour). The study was deleted and restarted.

Reading:
- Step A: the tuned setting ties the pin on f2 Calmar (1.738 vs 1.736) at much lower return and drawdown. The 1.47
  training score was mostly fit.
- Step B: all five top settings beat the pin on f3 (1.14–1.21 vs 1.04), with similar return and a smaller drawdown.
- Both steps push the same way: more gold (0.35–0.4), a stronger delivery filter (0.5–0.6), the ₹1 crore floor and
  fewer names (25–35). Gold and delivery sit at the top edge of their ranges, so the ranges were too narrow.
- Not adopted (pilot). The ₹1 crore floor was weaker at 50 bps and has less capacity (M33); any follow-up must carry
  the cost and capacity checks.
