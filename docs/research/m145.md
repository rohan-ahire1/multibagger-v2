# M145: a quality-screened value sleeve (pre-registered 2026-10-25, before any M145 backtest)

Twenty-eighth cycle of the autonomous loop. A follow-up with its own reason, not a fix of M144's numbers.

## Why

M144's value book lost 75.7% in 2010-13 by buying the cheapest stocks just before they collapsed (ARSSINFRA, ZYLOG,
CREWBOS, ADSL): value traps. The literature's answer to value traps is to require quality: Piotroski (2000, JAR, the
F-score: among cheap stocks, those with positive and improving profits beat the rest by about 7.5% a year); Novy-Marx
(2013, JFE: value works much better combined with profitability); Asness, Frazzini & Pedersen (2019, "Quality minus
junk"). Only quarterly net profit is available point in time (no balance sheet before XBRL), so the screen uses
profits only.

## Rule (`strategies/value/configs/m145_qvalue.yaml`, `ParamsM145.quality: stable_growth`)

M144's value book (top 25 by trailing earnings yield, M106's floors, equal weight, month-end), but a name is eligible
only if each of its last four quarters has a positive net profit and the four quarters sum to more than the four a
year earlier (eight first filings known at the decision; `fundamentals.stable_growth`). Nothing else changes.

## Candidates (dev 2010-01-04..2023-12-31, `nse_research@2026-10-25`)

`m145_q20` (M106 0.8 + quality value 0.2) and `m145_q30` (0.7 / 0.3), monthly rebalanced, momentum sleeve at offsets
0/5/10/15 (`m144_ref*`), and at 25 bps. The quality value book alone is information.

## The bar (LOOP.md, unchanged)

As M144: Calmar higher before and after tax at offset 0 and before tax at offsets 5/10/15; no fold lost; higher at 25
bps; the other weight holds; bootstrap ≥ 85%. Pre-screen first. No `--final`.

Prior: low. Value lagged on NSE in 2014-20, and the screen can only remove the worst traps. Expected fail. Variants
before M145: about 1,031; M145 adds 2.
