# M34: does the lower liquidity floor help the other books too?

Pre-registration (2026-10-01, Claude), written and committed before any run. Development period
2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`. Dataset `nse_research@2026-10-01b`. No new code.

## Why

M33 lowered the floor from ₹5 crore to ₹2 crore of 50-session average daily turnover on one book (gold20, 40 names,
inverse-vol) and Calmar rose from 0.712 to 0.865. If the gain comes from a real effect (momentum is stronger in less
traded stocks) and not from the particular names that one book happened to pick, it should show in the other books
too, as M23 did for gold.

## Runs (question `momentum-m34-liquidity-books`)

Each M23 config (gold20 on the other three books) moved to dataset `nse_research@2026-10-01b`, run twice: as it is
(₹5 crore, the reference) and with `min_adv_inr: 20000000`.

| Book | Reference config | ₹2 crore config |
|---|---|---|
| b1: 30 names, equal weight | `m34_gold20_b1` | `m34_adv2cr_gold20_b1` |
| b2: 40 names, equal weight | `m34_gold20_b2` | `m34_adv2cr_gold20_b2` |
| invvol_b1: 30 names, inverse-vol | `m34_gold20_invvol_b1` | `m34_adv2cr_gold20_invvol_b1` |

Read as: the floor generalizes if it raises whole-period Calmar on all three books. Each ₹2 crore config is also
judged by the usual bar against the research pin `momentum/m33_adv2cr_gold20_b2` (0.865): one that passes would be
checked like M33 (offsets, 25 bps, capacity, bootstrap) before any change of reference.

## Disclosure

All results to M33 are known, including that ₹1 crore was stronger at 10 bps but weaker at 50 bps. In M23, b1 gold20
had 0.697, b2 0.681, invvol_b1 0.708. No ₹2 crore run on these books has been made.
