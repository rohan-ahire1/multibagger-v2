# M143: scaled turnover (traded value / market cap) as a buy filter (pre-registered 2026-10-25, before any M143 backtest)

Twenty-sixth cycle of the autonomous loop.

## Idea and why it is not a retry

Lee & Swaminathan (2000, JF, "Price momentum and trading volume"): among past winners, high-turnover stocks
(shares traded / shares outstanding) earn less over the next one to five years than low-turnover winners. Late-stage,
crowded winners trade heavily and reverse. BacktestIndia (2026 blog, not peer-reviewed): Nifty 200 Momentum 30 names
split by "scaled turnover" (traded value / market cap), low half 19.4% vs high half 8.5% CAGR, Dec 2006 to Jun 2025.

Cycle 10 dropped this for one reason only: no history of shares outstanding for M106's small caps. M141's results
fetch now gives paid-up capital / face value per quarter for 97% of filings (spot checks: INFY, TCS, RELIANCE,
KPRMILL through their bonuses and splits). Related but different, both failed: M40 (delivery share, a speculation
measure on volume, kept in M106) and M53 (recent delivered volume vs its own past). Neither scales by company size.

## Rule

Turnover ratio at decision d = mean raw `turnover` over the last 50 sessions / market cap at d. Market cap = shares
from the latest first filing usable at d (paid-up / face value; quarter ended within 200 days) x raw close at d x
adj_factor(d) / adj_factor(quarter end), which carries the share count through later splits and bonuses
(`mb.data.fundamentals.turnover_ratio`). Among the decision's ranked (eligible) names with a ratio, names not held are
not bought when their ratio is in the top `drop_frac` (ties by symbol). Held names are kept as in M106; names without
a ratio are not affected. Everything else is M106. `ParamsM143.scaled_turnover`; tests in `tests/test_m141_results.py`.

Candidates: `m143_tr20` (drop_frac 0.2) and `m143_tr40` (0.4), offsets 0/5/10/15. References: m136_ref,
m141_ref_off5/10/15.
