# M141: quarterly results as buy filters (pre-registered 2026-10-24, before any M141 backtest)

Twenty-fourth cycle of the autonomous loop (`docs/research/LOOP.md`).

## Why a new data family

Cycles 10-23 found that price-only changes to M106 (ranking, weighting, buy filters, regime, stops) are spent, and
event filters (insider sells, deals, pledges, results reactions) remove winners that keep winning. The journal's
queue said: new data families only. M106 buys small and mid caps that are almost never F&O stocks (M118/M119), so
option or futures signals cannot reach it (a futures-basis idea was dropped this cycle for that reason). Company
fundamentals have not been tried at all in M1-M140.

New source: NSE's `corporates-financial-results` API lists every quarterly filing per symbol. Old-format filings
(to about 2018) link an HTML page of label / value rows in Rs lakh; newer ones link an XBRL file. Both give sales, net
profit and EPS back to about 2005 for every listed company (checked on KPRMILL and INFY). New:
`mb data fetch results configs/data/nse_results.yaml --dataset nse_research@2026-10-23` (standalone results, quarters
from 2007, symbols that met the price / ADV floors on some session from 2009: 2,649), `src/mb/sources/nse_results.py`,
`src/mb/data/fundamentals.py`. Raw files: `data/raw/nse_results/{listings,detail}` (immutable, gzip).

Point in time: each (symbol, quarter) uses its first filing; a filing counts from the day after NSE's filing date.
Later re-filings (restatements, the 2016 Ind-AS re-statements, late XBRL copies) are ignored.

## Sources

- Novy-Marx (2015, NBER w20984, "Fundamentally, momentum is fundamental momentum"): earnings surprises subsume price
  momentum in US cross-sectional regressions. Adjusting earnings momentum for past returns removes momentum's crashes.
- Chan, Jegadeesh & Lakonishok (1996, JF): price and earnings momentum each predict returns. Stocks strong on both do
  best.
- India: Harshita, Singh & Yadav (2018, Theoretical Economics Letters; 2002-2017, Fama-MacBeth): significant SUE drift
  that survives size, B/M, illiquidity and idiosyncratic-volatility controls. Against: Gupta & Dhusia (2021, 100 NSE firms,
  2014-18) find no drift.
- Loss-makers: Fama & French (2006, 2015) profitability. Asness, Frazzini & Pedersen (2019, "Quality minus junk"):
  unprofitable "junk" stocks have low later returns. Unprofitable small-cap winners are the lottery-like names behind
  momentum crashes (Daniel & Moskowitz 2016, high-beta past losers aside).

Prior from this project: buy filters on M106 move Calmar within the ±0.05-0.2 decision-day band (M120-M125, M134).
Earnings are a different kind of information from anything tried so far, so they are worth one test. Expected:
probably a fail.

## Rules (no new buys; held names are kept by the hold rule as in M106)

At decision d, a symbol's latest quarter is its newest first filing usable at d, if that quarter ended within
`max_age_days` = 200 calendar days of d. Otherwise, or when the needed quarters are missing, the name is not flagged
(it can be bought as in M106).

1. `m141_loss`: flagged when the latest quarter and the three before it (all known at d) sum to a net loss or zero.
2. `m141_yoy`: flagged when the latest quarter's net profit is below the same quarter's a year earlier (the sign of
   the seasonal random-walk surprise, Foster, Olsen & Shevlin 1984; no scaling, so nothing to fit).

Everything else is M106 (`m136_ref`): `ParamsM141.fundamentals` with `rule`, `max_age_days`, `raw_dir`. Code:
`mb.data.fundamentals.flags`, the nosell path in `targets()`. Tests: `tests/test_m141_results.py`.

## Information before any backtest (no returns)

Once the fetch is complete: (a) parse coverage by year (filings with a net profit / filings fetched); (b) the share of
`m136_ref`'s buys (offsets 0/5/10/15) with a usable latest quarter, and the share each rule flags. If either rule flags
fewer than 3% of buys, it is dropped without a backtest (too few to act, like the M137 note). If coverage of buys in
2010-13 is under 70%, f1 is reported but the verdict says so.

## The bar (LOOP.md, unchanged)

Against M106: (1) Calmar higher before and after tax at offset 0, and before tax at offsets 5/10/15; (2) no fold lost
(f1/f2/f3 at offset 0); (3) higher at 25 bps; (4) neighbours (`max_age_days` 120 / 300, and for `loss` two quarters
instead of four) do not collapse, and block bootstrap ≥ 85%. No `--final`.
Pre-screen first (`notebooks/prescreen.py`): any offset or fold lost ends the test.

Fail: any point above. Variants tried before M141: about 1,018; M141 adds 2 (neighbours only if the pre-screen passes).
