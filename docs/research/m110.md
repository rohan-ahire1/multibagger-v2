# M110: after-tax results and a tax-aware hold rule (2026-10-04)

Asked by the user: model Indian capital-gains tax, try a rule that cuts it, judge on before-tax and after-tax.

## Tax model (`src/mb/evaluation/tax.py`, `configs/tax/india_fy2026.yaml`)

Today's rules applied to every year: equity short-term 20%, long-term (held over 365 days) 12.5% above Rs 1.25 lakh a
year; GOLDBEES short-term at a 30% slab, long-term 12.5%; 4% cess; surcharge ignored. FIFO lots from the simulator's
fills (cost and proceeds net of charges). Set-off: short-term losses against any gain (highest rate first), long-term
losses against long-term gains; losses carry forward. Advance tax: the year's gains so far are paid up on 15 Jun,
15 Sep, 15 Dec and 15 Mar, settled at the year end (refund if later losses). The after-tax book is the pre-tax book
times k, cut by each payment. Dividends are inside adjusted prices, so they are taxed as gains (known limitation).
Unit tests: `tests/test_m110_tax.py`. Numbers: `notebooks/m110_tax.py` (re-simulates; checks the pre-tax curve against
the run record).

After-tax CAGR and DD come from the after-tax curve (tax paid basis); "whole+liq" also taxes the open lots at the end.

## Pre-registered rule (written before any M110 run)

`m110_taxhold.yaml` = M106 + a held name the hold buffer would drop is kept while: held 275 to 370 days since the
decision that first picked it, its adjusted close is above the one at that decision, and its rank is under 50. It is
sold at the first decision from day 371 (so the fill is past 365 days). It keeps its slot. One config; no tuning.

Information only (not a candidate): `m110_noresize.yaml` = M106 with `resize_held: false` (held names are not
trimmed monthly, so fewer partial short-term sales).

Pass: after-tax Calmar (tax-paid curve) above M106's on 2010-23 and on every fold, and before-tax Calmar at least 95%
of M106's on 2010-23 and on every fold. Expectation: small effect (at most ~Rs 4 lakh tax saved on the 29 exits).

## Results

Runs (question m110; report `reports/m110.md`): taxhold 20261004-034742-60eb30ad, noresize 20261004-034752-7d703098,
taxhold final 20261004-034910-6c1e5b46 (its one final). M106 final for comparison: 20261002-054052-94c22192 (M107).
Re-simulated pre-tax curves match every run record exactly.

| 2010-23 Calmar | whole | f1 | f2 | f3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M106 before tax | 1.50 | 2.26 | 2.20 | 1.68 |
| taxhold before tax | 1.51 | 2.41 | 2.21 | 1.69 |
| M106 after tax | 1.102 | 1.664 | 1.640 | 1.221 |
| taxhold after tax | 1.112 | 1.775 | 1.664 | 1.224 |
| noresize before / after tax | 1.29 / 1.04 | 1.57 / 1.21 | 1.98 / 1.65 | 1.48 / 1.17 |

taxhold passes the pre-registered bar (after-tax higher everywhere; before-tax not lower), but by a hair: +0.2 pts
after-tax CAGR (24.53% -> 24.74%), f3 Calmar +0.003. Stock exits 275-370 days after entry fell from 33 to 19.

Locked 2024-01-01..2026-09-28 (one look): M106 20.39% / DD 15.52% before tax, 15.44% after; taxhold 20.12% / 15.52%
before, 15.32% after. Slightly worse, within noise.

noresize (information): cuts tax paid by a third (Rs 44 lakh -> 30 lakh) but loses 3 pts of pre-tax CAGR, so it is
worse after tax too.

Verdict: tax costs the book about 5 to 5.5 pts of CAGR a year (30.1% -> 24.5% in 2010-23; 20.4% -> 15.4% in 2024-26),
mostly 20% short-term tax on a book that turns over 7x a year. The tax-hold rule's effect is too small to tell from
noise and is slightly negative on the locked years; it is not adopted. The paper book stays M106. Keep the after-tax
columns for every future candidate.
