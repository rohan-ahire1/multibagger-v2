# M96: robustness of M95's r50g_jr tranches (information)

## Question

M95's `blend/m95_r50g_jr_tranche_b2` passed the bar. Before it is weighed for adoption:

1. **Neighbours** (tranches): filter 150 and 250 sessions (±50); risk-off gross 0.25 and 0.75 (a quarter or three
   quarters to gold, ±0.25). Does the pass survive one step in each direction?
2. **Bootstrap** (M20 settings): vs the pin's tranches and vs M89's r50g tranches.
3. **Stitched walk-forward 2014–23** (M52): A / B / C settings with the r50g_jr rule.

Nothing is adopted from this milestone; adoption is the user's call.

**Reading.** r50g_jr tranches are the strongest candidate so far on the formal bar: they pass, pass at 25 bps, have the
best 2007–09 score, and the bootstrap gives a 92% chance of a higher Calmar and 98% of a higher CAGR than the pin's

depends on the exact setting (2 of 4 neighbours miss it). On the whole period the family is robustly ~1.6–1.9 vs ~1.1.
