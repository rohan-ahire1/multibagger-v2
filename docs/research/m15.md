# M15: does the inverse-vol result hold on the rename-linked data?

Pre-registration, written and committed before any M15 run (2026-09-30, Claude working alone overnight at the user's
request). Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`. Dataset `nse_research@2026-09-30` (M14).

## Why

M11 recommended inverse-vol weights on the strength of the paired timing check (it won at all eight decision offsets);
M13 checked it under higher costs. Both ran on `nse_research@2026-09-29f`, which lost a renamed stock's history. M14
fixed that and moved fold 2 by up to −0.16 Calmar; the offset-0 comparison still favours inverse-vol, but the other
seven offsets and the cost check have not been re-run. M15 re-runs them. No new idea; no option is added.

## Runs

Configs are the M14 configs (`m14_linked_{b1,b2,invvol_b1,invvol_b2}`) with one field changed:

| Config | Question | Change |
|---|---|---|
| `m15_off{5,10,15}_{b1,b2}` | `momentum-m15-paired` | equal weight, `schedule.decision_offset_sessions` 5 / 10 / 15 |
| `m15_invvol_off{5,10,15}_{b1,b2}` | `momentum-m15-paired` | inverse-vol, the same offsets |
| `m15_slip25_{b1,b2,invvol_b1,invvol_b2}` | `momentum-m15-costs` | market `nse_cash_slip25` (as M13) |

Offset 0 is the M14 pins.

## Decision rule

The recommendation (book 2 with inverse-vol) stands if both hold:

1. Paired timing: inverse-vol beats equal weight on whole-period Calmar at seven or more of the eight (book, offset)
   pairs, offset 0 included (M11's bar: losing at two or more means not robust).
2. Costs: inverse-vol beats equal weight on whole-period Calmar on both books, and on at least two of three folds on
   each (M13's bar).

If either fails, the recommendation is reported as weakened and the paper draft is marked not to deploy. Results are
reported either way; nothing is deployed.

## Disclosure

- The offset-0 results on this dataset are known (M14). The other runs are not.
- The eight M11 paired runs on the old dataset all favoured inverse-vol; this checks whether that survives the data fix.

## Amendment (2026-09-30, before any run below)

**What happened.** The first pass ran all 16 configs on `nse_research@2026-09-30`. Eight finished (`m15_off{5,10}_b1`,
`m15_invvol_off{5,10}_b1`, the four `m15_slip25_*`); eight were refused by the data gate (`m15_off15_b1`,
`m15_invvol_off15_b1`, all six book-2 offset runs), e.g. `DataGateError: 1 trade(s) held through an unexplained price
jump: GFLLIMITED 2019-04-12..2019-08-19 jump 2019-08-06 x0.109`. The metrics of the eight finished runs were not read
before this amendment. Two of them were accidentally run a second time (`m15_off10_b1` 20260929-194033-be39c2d8,
`m15_off5_b1` 20260929-194058-22fcc5f3; same config hashes as 20260929-193823-be39c2d8 and 20260929-193850-22fcc5f3).

**Cause.** Linking gives a renamed stock its earlier bars, which also makes an old unexplained jump rankable when it
falls just after the rename. Four jumps in the dev period sit within 400 days after a link; each is a real corporate
action the feed has without a factor:

| Symbol | Jump | Was | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| SWELECTES | 2012-07-17 ×0.526 | NUMERICPW | ₹120 interim dividend (feed parsed only the ₹3 final) |
| GFLLIMITED | 2019-08-06 ×0.109 | GUJFLUORO | demerger of the fluorochemicals business (FLUOROCHEM) |
| BORORENEW | 2020-03-06 ×0.264 | BOROSIL | demerger of the consumer glass business (Borosil Ltd) |
| DEEPENR | 2020-11-10 ×0.615 | DEEPIND | demerger of the oil and gas services business (DEEPINDS) |

**Fix.** Four `manual` rows in `configs/data/corporate_actions_overrides.csv`, factors set the way the KPIT and TATACHEM
demergers were (ex-date open / cum-date close) and the MAJESCO dividend was (1 − dividend / prior close). A new dataset
`nse_research@2026-09-30b` is built from the same data config (`nse_research_linked.yaml`); `2026-09-30` is not
changed. Expected: four fewer unexplained jumps, otherwise the same as `2026-09-30`. The CSV is shared, so later
`nse_daily` builds read the rows too; all four are before 2023, where `nse_daily` has no bars.

**Runs.** Each M15 config and the four M14 configs are copied with only `dataset` changed to `nse_research@2026-09-30b`
(prefix `m15b_`; the M14 copies are `m15b_off0_*`, `m15b_invvol_off0_*`). Questions `momentum-m15b-paired` (16 runs:
2 books × 4 offsets × 2 weightings) and `momentum-m15b-costs` (4 runs). The decision rule above applies unchanged to these
runs, with offset 0 now the `m15b_off0` runs. The eight first-pass runs and the M14 pins are reported alongside, not
used for the decision.
