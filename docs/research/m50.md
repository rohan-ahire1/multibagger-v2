# M50: is the new pin a knife-edge? One-step neighbours of `m49_b_b2`

Pre-registration (2026-10-02, Claude), committed before any M50 run. Development period 2010–2023, dataset
`nse_research@2026-10-02b`, offset 0. Information only: nothing is adopted from M50.

## Why

B was the best of ~230 search trials on 2010–17. A setting chosen that way can sit on a narrow peak. If its one-step
neighbours score far lower, the 1.125 is mostly luck and the forward expectation should be cut; if they stay well
above the old pin (0.913), the gain comes from the region, not the point.

## What is read

Each neighbour's Calmar and folds. Summary: the median and minimum of the 12. "Robust" if the median is above 1.0 and
the minimum above the old pin (0.913).

Disclosure: M42 step B's top 5 were near-copies of B with the same score, so very close neighbours are known to be flat;
these steps are larger.
