# M99: the 200-day filter on market breadth

## Question

M89 (Nifty 50) and M95 (Nifty Next 50) show the filter works better the closer its index is to what the book holds.
The closest possible signal is the book's own universe: the share of eligible stocks (the ranking's series, price,
liquidity and fund rules) that close at or above their own 200-day average ("percentage of stocks above the 200-day
average", a standard breadth measure; Zweig 1986). Risk-off when fewer than half are above: half the book to gold, as
in r50g. It needs no index and covers small and mid caps.

New code: `regime.breadth_min_frac` with `benchmark_symbol: UNIVERSE` (`ParamsM99`); refused by paper.
