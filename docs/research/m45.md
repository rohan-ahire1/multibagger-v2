# M45: intermediate momentum (months 12 to 7)

Pre-registration (2026-10-02, Claude), committed before any M45 run. Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31; no
`--final`. Dataset `nse_research@2026-10-01c`. Base: the pin `momentum/m40_deliv40_gold20_b2`.

## Why

Novy-Marx (2012, *Is momentum really momentum?*, JFE) finds that the return from 12 to 7 months ago predicts returns
better than the most recent months (6 to 2). If that holds in India, ranking on the older half of the year should keep
the return and drop some of the crowded, recently-hot names that fell hardest in March 2020.

Also considered and rejected before any run: industry momentum (Moskowitz & Grinblatt 1999). The only industry map
is today's Nifty 500 list (524 names), so it would leak today's index membership into the past.

## What is compared

Standard adoption check against the pin (question `momentum-m45`); then, only if it passes, the four decision offsets
and 25 bps (question `momentum-m45-checks`), the M40 rules: win at least 3 of 4 offsets and pass at 25 bps.

## Expected

Expected: lower CAGR (staler ranking, more names that already turned) and likely a fail; Indian evidence on 12-7 vs 12-2
is thin. One config only.
