# M132: sell picks moved to trade-to-trade between decisions (pre-registered 2026-10-23, before any M132 backtest)

Fourteenth cycle of the autonomous loop.

## Idea and sources

NSE moves stocks it suspects of manipulation or excess speculation from the rolling EQ series to trade-to-trade
(BE / BZ: delivery only, often a tighter price band). M106 only trades EQ, but it finds out at the next month-end and
sells then. Parmar & Chaturvedula (2017, *Accounting and Finance Research* 6(1), "trade-for-trade" event study):
large positive abnormal returns in the 30 days before the shift, negative abnormal returns and higher volatility in
the 30 days after. A rule that sells at the next open after the move should avoid part of that.

Disclosure (an outcome was looked at before this pre-registration): an information count on the M106 reference run
`20261022-194129-d756637e` (2010-23) found 55 holdings that moved off EQ between decisions, mostly 2021-23; from the
close of the first non-EQ session to the month-end exit their adjusted close fell 4.0% on average (median −4.0%,
64% negative). With ~3% weight each, that is at most ~0.5 points of CAGR over 14 years before the fill gap at the
move; the effect on Calmar is unknown. This candidate is therefore weaker evidence than a blind one.

## Rule

Between two decisions, the first session on which a held stock pick (not GOLDBEES) has rows only outside
`allowed_series` ([EQ]) adds a target that sells it at the next open; the other holdings are left as they are
(keep_size) and the proceeds stay in cash until the next decision. A move on the decision session itself is left to
that decision (as M106). Code: `ExitsM132.series_exit`, `ParamsM132`, `series_exits` (`targets.py`);
`tests/test_m132_series_exit.py`. Paper support (`src/mb/live`) would be needed before any deployment.

## The bar (LOOP.md, unchanged)

Against `m120_ref*`: (1) Calmar higher before and after tax at offset 0, before tax at offsets 5/10/15; (2) no fold
lost (ties count as not lost); (3) higher at 25 bps; (4) bootstrap ≥ 85%. No `--final` run.

Fail: any point above. Expected: tiny (+0.1-0.3 points CAGR, a few events a year, mostly 2021-23). Variants tried
before M132: about 1,009; M132 adds 1.
