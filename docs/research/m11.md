# M11 research: when and how much, not which names

Pre-registration, written and committed before any M11 run (2026-09-30, Claude working alone overnight at the user's
request). Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; the locked period is not touched (no `--final`). Dataset
`nse_research@2026-09-29f`. Adoption metric: Calmar (`configs/baselines.yaml`).

## Why

M5, M8 and M10 changed which names the book picks (17 variants); the plain vol-adjusted 12-1 rank was never beaten on
every fold. M11 leaves the pick list alone and changes three other things, each aimed at the book's drawdown (about 40%):

| Idea | Source | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| **Tranches** | Hoffstein, Faber & Braun (2020), *Rebalance Timing Luck* (Newfound Research); the overlapping portfolios of Jegadeesh & Titman (1993) | A monthly book's result depends on which day it trades. Splitting it into staggered parts removes that luck and smooths the curve at no cost to expected return |
| **Stop-loss** | Han, Zhou & Zhu (2016), *Taming Momentum Crashes: A Simple Stop-Loss Strategy* | A 10% stop on each momentum stock, reset each month, cuts the worst monthly loss sharply and raises the Sharpe ratio |
| **Inverse-vol weights** | Barroso & Santa-Clara (2015), *Momentum Has Its Moments*, J. Financial Economics (the 6-month realised-vol window) | Momentum risk is predictable from recent volatility; here applied across names: calmer names get more weight |

The M8 market-volatility target (a whole-book gross scale) failed; inverse-vol weights keep the book fully invested and
only change the mix inside it.

## New options (a `ParamsM11` model; older configs keep their model and hash)

- `schedule.decision_offset_sessions`: the decision date is this many sessions before each month's last session (0 is
  the month-end, as now). Trades still happen at the next session's open.
- `exits.stop_loss_frac`: when a held name closes at or below (1 − this) × its price at the last rebalance session (the
  open it was bought or resized at; for a name the rebalance left alone, that session's open), it is sold at the next
  session's open. The cash waits for the next rebalance. On a rebalance session the rebalance runs instead of stops.
- `portfolio.weighting`: `equal` (as now) or `inverse_vol`: weight ∝ 1 / stdev of daily log returns over
  `portfolio.inverse_vol_sessions` sessions ending at the decision date.

Paper trading refuses all three (not ignored), so the running books cannot pick them up by accident.

## Timing diagnostic (question `momentum-m11-timing`; measured, not adopted)

`m11_offset{5,10,15}_b1` and `_b2`: each book with the decision date 5, 10 and 15 sessions before the month-end. The
spread of their Calmars against the pin says how much of any 0.02–0.05 difference seen in M5–M10 is just the trading day.
They are also the tranche sleeves below.

| Config | Question | Change |
|---|---|---|

| `*_b2` | `momentum-m11-b2` | The same three on book 2 |

Bold numbers are the papers' own choices, fixed here and not tuned. The tranche blend runs each sleeve at 25% of the
capital, so it pays four sets of per-sale fees and does not net trades between sleeves: slightly worse than a real
tranche book, never better.

## Decision rule

Per book, the usual bar: beat the pin and both baselines on Calmar on the whole period and on each fold. An idea is
**recommended** only if (1) it passes on both books, (2) its neighbours also beat book 1's pin on whole-period Calmar
(tag `momentum-m11-neighbours`; tranches: 2 sleeves at offsets 0, 10 and 3 sleeves at 0, 7, 14; stop: 0.05 and 0.20;
inverse-vol: 63 and 252 sessions), and (3) the gain does not come from one fold alone. Neighbour configs are written
only for ideas that pass (1). If more than one idea is recommended, the one with the higher book-1 whole-period Calmar
is the candidate. No combinations in M11. A recommended idea joins paper trading only on the user's say-so; the running
books are not changed.

Also reported, not used to decide: CAGR, max drawdown, turnover, per-fold Calmar, stop exits per year.

## Disclosure

- Momentum variants tried on this period before M11: 17 (M5 5, M8 6, M10 6), plus two unread dataset-e M10 runs.
  M11 adds 6 (plus neighbours). After this many tries a small win is likely luck; forward paper trading is the test.
- The references' numbers are known. No M11 option has been run on this data before this note was committed.
- Han, Zhou & Zhu use US data, daily lows and long-short portfolios; here the stop uses the close and sells at the next
  open, so a gap down is taken in full.

## Amendment 1 (2026-09-30, after the six variants and the inverse-vol neighbours were read): paired timing check

The timing diagnostic moved book 1's Calmar from 0.53 to 0.72 with the trading day alone, a spread larger than the
inverse-vol gain. So before any recommendation, one extra check, fixed here before it is run: inverse-vol (126
sessions) at decision offsets 5, 10 and 15 on both books (`m11_invvol_off{5,10,15}_{b1,b2}`, tag
`momentum-m11-paired`), each compared with the equal-weight run at the same offset (`m11_offset*`). This check can only
lower confidence: if inverse-vol loses whole-period Calmar at two or more of the six paired offsets, the idea is
reported as not robust and not recommended.
