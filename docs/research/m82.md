# M82: buy a stopped name back when it recovers

## Question

The 10% stop's cost is in bull years (2014–17, 2021): a name dips 10%, is sold, recovers, and the money waits in cash
until month-end. Stop-loss rules with a re-entry condition (Kaminski & Lo 2014, "When do stop-loss rules stop
losses?"; Lo & Remorov 2017) keep the protection in lasting falls but get back in after a whipsaw. Rule: a name stopped
since the last rebalance that closes back at or above its stop reference (the rebalance-session open) is bought back
at the next open with up to that stop's proceeds; it can be stopped again under the same rule.

| Candidate | Change from the pin |
|---|---|
| `m82_reent_b2` | 10% stop with re-entry at the reference price |

One variant, no new number. New engine option in ParamsM82 (`exits.stop_reentry`). Known before: M72–M81. Same checks
and bar as M73–M81 (main bar at offset 0; offsets 3 of 4; 25 bps; 2007–09 mean ≥ 0.284; tranches above the pin's
tranches 1.105; 1.590 / 2.199 / 1.312). If it does well, the half-blend form (as M78) follows as a separate step.
