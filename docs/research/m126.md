# M126: the Next 50 filter graded over several moving-average lengths (pre-registered 2026-10-23, before any M126 run)

Seventh cycle of the autonomous loop.

## Idea and sources

M106 halves the stock book into gold when JUNIORBEES closes below its 200-session average. One length is one bet on
one speed: trend-following results depend heavily on the chosen lookback, and averaging several lengths
("specification diversification", trend ensembles) keeps most of the protection while cutting the luck of the single
choice (Zakamulin, *Market Timing with Moving Averages*, 2017; Hoffstein, Faber & Braun 2020 on timing luck; Newfound
Research on ensemble trend signals; Levine & Pedersen 2016, *FAJ*: moving-average crossovers and time-series momentum
are the same family over a spread of horizons). The deepest M106 drawdown is the slow 2022 bear (20.1%, Jan–May
2022), where filter speed matters.

Disclosure: M109's information nudges (seen 2026-10-03) put SMA 160 at 1.66 and SMA 240 at 1.67 against 200's 1.50, at
offset 0 only. That is why the filter's length looked fragile; the ensemble lengths below are round numbers chosen
symmetric around 200, not those two values. M97 graded two indices (Nifty 50 and Next 50) at one length and failed f1;
this grades one index over several lengths.

## Rule

Signals: JUNIORBEES adj_close ≥ its n-session simple average, for each n in the set. With a fraction f of them risk-on,
the stock book's gross is f + (1 − f) × 0.5 and the rest goes to GOLDBEES (M97's grading, M106's risk-off share).
Code: `RegimeM126`/`ParamsM126` (`params.py`), the signal list in `targets.py`, `tests/test_m126_sma_ensemble.py`.

## The bar (LOOP.md, unchanged)

Against `m120_ref*`: (1) Calmar higher before and after tax at offset 0, before tax at offsets 5/10/15; (2) no fold
lost; (3) higher at 25 bps; (4) neighbours (ens3 at 125/200/275 and 175/200/225) hold, bootstrap ≥ 85%. If both pass,
the higher four-offset mean wins. No `--final` run.

Fail: any point above. Expected: moderate chance; M109 says the neighbourhood of 200 is above 200 itself, but the
grading also adds partial risk-off months that cost return. Variants tried before M126: about 1,002; M126 adds 2.
