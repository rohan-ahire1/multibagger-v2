# M127: momentum-score tilt on M106's weights (pre-registered 2026-10-23, before any M127 backtest)

Eighth cycle of the autonomous loop.

## Idea and sources

NSE's own momentum indices (Nifty 200 Momentum 30, Nifty Midcap150 Momentum 50; NSE index methodology) and MSCI's
Momentum Indexes weight each stock by its normalised momentum score times its size: the score's z-score Z, winsorised
at ±3, becomes 1 + Z when Z > 0 and 1 / (1 − Z) otherwise. M106 holds 25 names at inverse-volatility weights and gives
its rank-1 name the same treatment as its rank-25 name (apart from volatility). M58/M64 (top 20 / 15) showed that more
concentration in the best names raised return but failed f2; a score tilt is the gentler form, keeping 25 names but
leaning toward the strongest. Weighting by signal strength is standard in factor portfolios (e.g. Israel, Jiang & Ross
2017, "Craftsmanship alpha", on signal-weighted vs equal-weighted construction).

## Rule

At each decision, Z = (score − mean) / sd (population) of M106's ranking score (273/14-session return over volatility)
across that decision's ranked names (after M106's universe and delivery filters), clipped at ±3. Each picked name's
inverse-volatility weight is multiplied by 1 + Z (Z > 0) or 1 / (1 − Z), then normalised as before; gold share and the
risk-off switch are unchanged. Code: `PortfolioM127`/`ParamsM127`, `score_tilts` (`targets.py`),
`tests/test_m127_score_tilt.py`.
