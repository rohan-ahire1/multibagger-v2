# M105: what the Next 50 filter does without the stop (information)

## Question

M95 = the pin's settings with three changes: no fixed gold, a 10% stop, and the JUNIORBEES filter (half to gold when
below its 200-day average). How much does the filter add on its own? Two decompositions:

| Config | Change from `m95_r50g_jr*` |
|---|---|
| `m105_g25_jr*` | the pin plus the filter: `fixed_weights: {GOLDBEES: 0.25}`, `stop_loss_frac: null` |
| `m105_g0_jr*` | no fixed gold, no stop, the filter only |

Information; both still face the bar. Disclosure: M95–M104 seen first.

**Reading.**
- The filter alone on the pin (keep its 25% gold, no stop) passes the bar at month-end (offset 0) at 5 and 25 bps,
  and in tranches at 5 and 25 bps; it beats the pin at every decision day on the whole period (offset 5 by little:
  1.086 vs 1.101 fails; its 27% drawdown is the 2020 crash, which the month-end filter missed on that schedule).
  It is the simplest passing change found: one rule, no stop, about the same CAGR as the pin (29–30%) with drawdown
  cut from 26–27% to 20–22%.
- The filter alone without gold makes the most money (36–37% CAGR) but keeps a 27% drawdown.
- Adding the 10% stop (M95) is worth a further 0.3 of Calmar in tranches (1.34 → 1.66), mostly by cutting drawdown.
- Practical: the paper book would need only the gold switch (`regime.risk_off_symbol`) for `g25_jr`, against the stop
  and the gold switch for M95.
