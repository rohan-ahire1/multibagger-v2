# M57: decide every 5 or 10 sessions instead of monthly, with a hold buffer

Pre-registered 2026-10-02, before any code or run.

## Idea

M37, M54 and M56 show that the month-end decision day moves Calmar by ±0.1 (timing luck). Deciding more often
spreads each month's decision over several days and reacts sooner to a crash, at a cost in trading. A hold buffer
keeps that cost down: a held name stays while its rank is within `hold_rank_max`, so most weekly decisions only
replace names that fell far. Novy-Marx & Velikov (2016) find frequent rebalancing with a band keeps most of
momentum's gross return net of costs; Hoffstein, Faber & Braun (2020) show shorter rebalance intervals cut timing luck.

## Change

`schedule.every_sessions` (null = monthly as now) and `schedule.phase_session`: decide on the sessions of
[start, end] with index ≡ phase (mod every), counting from the period's first session. `decision_offset_sessions`
must be 0 with it. Everything else (ranking, inverse-vol weights, gold top-up, resizing) runs at each decision as now.
