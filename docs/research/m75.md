# M75: park stop-loss proceeds in gold

## Question

In M73/M74 the stop's proceeds wait in cash (no yield) until the next decision date. Han, Zhou & Zhu (2016) put stopped
money into the risk-free asset; here the natural safe asset is the book's own gold sleeve (GOLDBEES), which is what the
book holds for crash cover anyway. New engine option `exits.stop_proceeds_to` (ParamsM75): stop proceeds buy that
fixed-weight symbol at the same open, the next rebalance trims it back, and the symbol itself is never stopped (it is the
safe asset; in M73 gold could be stopped too, rarely).

| Candidate | Change from the pin |
|---|---|
| `m75_s10_park_b2` | 10% stop, proceeds to GOLDBEES (M73's stop10 + parking) |
| `m75_g10s10_park_b2` | gold 0.10, 10% stop, proceeds to GOLDBEES (M74's g10s10 + parking) |

Two variants. Known before: M73/M74 results without parking. Expected: small changes; most help in slow bears (2008,
2011) where stopped cash sat idle for weeks while gold rose.

All checks generated and run with the candidates (offsets, 25 bps at four offsets, 2007–09 at four offsets, tranche
blends for each). Bar as M74: the pin (1.125; 1.598 / 2.072 / 1.362) and baselines, then offsets 3 of 4, 25 bps, 2007–09
mean ≥ 0.284, tranche form above the pin's tranches (1.105; 1.590 / 2.199 / 1.312). Also compared, as information,
with the same book without parking (M73 stop10, M74 g10s10).

**Verdict: both fail** (f2 at offset 0; f2 in tranches; worse than the same book without parking almost everywhere).

Why: the money is parked in gold right when stocks crash, and in the two worst crashes gold fell too (March 2020, a
liquidity sell-off; April–June 2022). Stop tranches' March 2020 loss goes from 17.8% to 20.4%, 2022 from 17.7% to
19.9%. Only the slow 2008 bear benefits a little (0.415 → 0.438). Cash is the better place for stopped money here.
