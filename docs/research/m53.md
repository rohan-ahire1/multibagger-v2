# M53: the high-volume return premium, on delivered volume

Pre-registration (2026-10-02, Claude), committed before any M53 run. Development period 2010–2023, dataset
`nse_research@2026-10-02b`. Reference: the pin `momentum/m49_b_b2` (run 20261002-000607-0103cc7f; Calmar 1.125, folds
1.60 / 2.07 / 1.36; offsets 5/10/15 1.101 / 1.055 / 1.142).

## Why

Gervais, Kaniel & Mingelgrin (2001, *The High-Volume Return Premium*, J. Finance 56(3)) find that stocks with unusually
high trading volume over a recent period, relative to their own history, earn higher returns over the following month
or more; low-volume stocks earn less. The explanation is visibility: a volume shock draws attention and new buyers.
M40 showed delivered volume (shares actually taken into demat) is a cleaner signal than total volume on the NSE. Here,
among the names the pin would consider, those whose recent delivered volume is lowest relative to their own past year
are dropped.

## Option (new `UniverseM53.abnormal_delivery`, null = off)

ratio = (sum of `deliv_qty` over the last `recent_sessions` rows up to the decision date / `recent_sessions`) /
(sum over the `base_sessions` rows before those / `base_sessions`), missing delivery records counting as 0. A name
with a zero base is not eligible. After the delivery-share drop, the `drop_frac` share (rounded up) of the remaining
names with the lowest ratio are dropped.

| Config | Change from the pin |
|---|---|
| `m53_control_b2` | `max_gap_days: null`, `abnormal_delivery: null` (must equal the pin exactly) |
| `m53_abd25_b2` | recent 21, base 252, drop 0.25 |
| `m53_abd50_b2` | recent 21, base 252, drop 0.50 |

21 and 252 (a month against the year before) are fixed, not tuned. Question `momentum-m53` (2 variants).

## Adoption (as M49)

(a) the bar against the pin at offset 0; (b) beats the pin at 3 of 4 offsets; (c) passes (a) at 25 bps against
`m49_b_slip25_b2` (run 20261002-000437-237b5d10); (d) capacity p95 ≤ 1% at ₹10 lakh. Highest four-offset mean if both
pass. (b)–(d) only for configs passing (a).
