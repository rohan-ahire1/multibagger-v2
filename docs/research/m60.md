# M60: past the search boundary on the delivery filter (drop 85% / 90%)

Pre-registered 2026-10-02, before any M60 run.

## Why

M42's search allowed `delivery_drop_frac` up to 0.8 and all three walk-forward steps chose 0.8, the edge. A setting
chosen at the edge of its range says the best value may lie past it. M50 showed the step down (0.7) costs 0.07. The
idea is Lee & Swaminathan (2000): winners with low speculative (non-delivered) trading continue longer; a stricter
filter keeps only the names bought for delivery.

## Runs (question `momentum-m60`, against `momentum/m49_b_b2`)

| Config | `delivery_drop_frac` |
|---|---|
| `m60_deliv85_b2` | 0.85 |
| `m60_deliv90_b2` | 0.90 |

Bar as M49 (whole period and every fold vs the pin and both baselines; 3 of 4 offsets; 25 bps; capacity p95 ≤ 1%,
which matters more here since fewer names qualify), plus the 2007–09 four-offset mean not below the pin's (0.284).
No other variants.
