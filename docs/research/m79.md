# M79: stop only in a falling market

## Question

The 10% stop (M73) fires on 700+ holdings over 2010–23, many of them single-name dips in rising markets (2014,
2017, 2021), where the money then idles until month-end; that is where its return cost sits (2021: +90% vs the pin's
+117% in tranches). Han, Zhou & Zhu (2016) motivate the stop by momentum crashes, which are market-wide (Daniel &
Moskowitz 2016: crashes come in market rebounds after falls, and in market falls). Condition the stop on the market:
a held name is stopped (same 10% rule) only on a day when NIFTYBEES also closes at or below (1 − g) × its open at the
last rebalance.

| Candidate | Change from the pin |
|---|---|
| `m79_mkt0_b2` | 10% stop, only while NIFTYBEES is below its rebalance open (g = 0: no new number) |
| `m79_mkt5_b2` | 10% stop, only while NIFTYBEES is 5% or more below its rebalance open (crash months only) |

Two variants. New engine option in ParamsM79 (`exits.stop_market_symbol`, `exits.stop_market_frac`). Known before:
M72–M78. Same checks and bar as M73–M78 (offsets 3 of 4, 25 bps, 2007–09 mean ≥ 0.284, tranches above the pin's
tranches 1.105; 1.590 / 2.199 / 1.312). If a candidate does well in tranches, the half-blend form (as M78) follows as a
separate milestone.
