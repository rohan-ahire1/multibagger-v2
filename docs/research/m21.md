# M21: a stop on the whole book

Pre-registration, written and committed before the code is used for any run (2026-09-30, Claude working alone overnight
at the user's request for a higher Calmar). Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`.

## Why

The reference book (`momentum/m19_gold20_invvol_b2`, Calmar 0.712) has its worst drawdown in the March 2020 crash
(peak 2020-02-20, trough 2020-03-23, −32%). A month-end rule cannot react to a fall that happens inside one month: that
is why M8's market-volatility target left the max DD unchanged. M11's stop-loss acted on each name separately and sold
winners on ordinary noise. A stop on the book as a whole reacts only when many picks fall together, which is what a
crash is; the literature on stop-loss rules (Kaminski & Lo 2014) finds they help when losses cluster.

## The rule (`ExitsM21.book_stop_frac`, a new config model `ParamsM21`)

After each decision close, the picks are followed as if held from that close at their target weights (adjusted
closes; gold and any other fixed symbol left out). At the first close where that value is at or below
(1 − `book_stop_frac`) × its highest close since the decision, every pick is sold at the next open with normal costs.
The gold sleeve is topped back to 20% of equity at that open; the rest waits in cash (earning the market's idle-cash
yield) until the next decision date, where the book is rebuilt as usual. At most one stop per month. The hold buffer
does not change (it has no effect in book 2, where `hold_rank_max` = `top_n`). Paper trading refuses the option.

## Runs (question `momentum-m21-bstop`, against the pin `momentum/m19_gold20_invvol_b2`)

| Config | Is |
|---|---|
| `m21_gold20_bstop10_b2` | `m19_gold20_invvol_b2` + `book_stop_frac: 0.10` |
| `m21_gold20_bstop15_b2` | `m19_gold20_invvol_b2` + `book_stop_frac: 0.15` |

Two thresholds, chosen now as round numbers. Adoption as always: beat the pin and both baselines on Calmar, whole
period and every fold. If one passes, it is also run at the M15 decision offsets (5, 10 and 15 sessions before the
month-end) against the gold20 offset runs from M19, adopted only if it wins at most of them, and then at 25 bps
slippage against `m20_gold20_slip25_b2`.

Also reported: the number of stops, how many were followed by the book's picks rising by the next decision date
(whipsaws), CAGR, max DD and where the worst drawdown moves.

## Disclosure

- Known before: the shape of the 2020 crash (about five weeks, most of it in March), which a 10–15% book stop would
  have cut partway through. That is exactly the kind of known event a rule can be fitted to; the fold results and the
  offset check are there to show whether it helps outside 2020.
- M11's per-name stop-loss failed; M8's month-end volatility target failed.
- Expected weak spot: sharp dips that recover within the month (2016 demonetisation, 2022), where the stop sells low
  and misses the rebound.
