# M74: winning back the stop's CAGR

## Question

M73's 10% stop cut drawdowns robustly (tranches: 17.8% max DD vs 26.7%) but cost about 2.5 points of CAGR. Two ways to
win it back, fixed now:

| Candidate | Change from the pin | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `m74_t20s10_b2` | top 20 (as M58) + 10% stop | M58's top 20 adds ~2 points of CAGR; the stop may contain its deeper drawdowns |
| `m74_g10s10_b2` | gold 0.10 instead of 0.25 + 10% stop | gold was the crash cover before the stop; less gold means more stocks in bull years |

Two variants. Known before: M58, M73 results; nothing about these combinations.

All checks are generated and run now with the candidates: offsets 5/10/15, 25 bps (all four offsets), 2007–09 (all
four), and the four-tranche blends (development, 25 bps, 2007–09).

## Bar

Usual bar against the pin (1.125; 1.598 / 2.072 / 1.362) and baselines. A passer needs 3 of 4 offsets above the pin's
(1.125 / 1.101 / 1.055 / 1.142); 25 bps above 1.078 and folds 1.498 / 1.971 / 1.310; 2007–09 mean ≥ 0.284; and the tranche
form above the pin's tranches (1.105; 1.590 / 2.199 / 1.312).

Also reported (information, the user asked for higher CAGR too): tranche CAGR against the pin tranches' 29.46%.
