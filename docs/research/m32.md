# M32: conditional volatility targeting of the stock book

Pre-registration (2026-10-01, Claude), written and committed before the code is used for any run. Development period
2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`. Dataset `nse_research@2026-10-01` (M31).

## Why

Every long-only book's max drawdown is March 2020. Momentum crashes come in high-volatility states (Daniel &
Moskowitz 2016), and volatility clusters, so a month that starts with high volatility is more likely to be a bad
one. Bongaerts, Kang & van Dijk, "Conditional Volatility Targeting" (Financial Analysts Journal 76(4), 2020), show
that scaling exposure all the time (Moreira & Muir 2017) does not reliably help equity markets and can deepen
drawdowns, but scaling **only in the extreme volatility states** consistently cut drawdowns and tail risk, with low
turnover, in the ten largest equity markets and in momentum factors across regions. Their rule, per month t:

- predicted volatility = realised volatility of month t − 1 (equally weighted stdev of daily returns);
- state: sort the realised volatility of all months up to t − 1; if month t − 1 is in the top quintile, the state is
  high (bottom quintile: low; otherwise medium);
- exposure = min(target / predicted, L_max) in an extreme state, 1 otherwise; target = the long-term realised
  volatility from all daily returns before month t.

This book cannot borrow, so L_max = 1: only the high state does anything.

M8's conventional rule (invest min(1, 20% / NIFTYBEES 63-session volatility) every month, `m8_voltarget_dev_d`, 0.59)
failed. This is different in three ways: it scales only in the top-quintile state, it measures the book's own
volatility (not the market's), and it uses one month of data.

## The rule (`PortfolioM32.vol_states`, a new config model `ParamsM32`)

1. A **shadow book** is the same strategy with no fixed weights and no scaling, run from the panel's first session
   (2005-01-03) at full investment, with the market's costs (the M27 machinery).
2. At each decision date d of the shadow, its volatility v(d) = stdev of daily log returns of shadow equity over the
   `vol_sessions` sessions to d (included). Only decision dates whose window starts after the shadow's first invested
   decision count.
3. At a decision date of the run: the state is high if v(d) ≥ the `high_quantile` quantile (numpy, linear) of v over
   all counted shadow decision dates up to d (included). Fewer than `min_history_months` counted dates is an error.
   Target = stdev of all daily log shadow returns from the first invested decision to d. Scale k = min(1, target /
   v(d)) in the high state, else 1.
4. The picks' combined weight is multiplied by k; fixed weights (GOLDBEES 20%) are unchanged; the rest is cash. Held
   names are resized (`resize_held` must be true).

## Runs (question `momentum-m32-cvt`, against the pin `momentum/m19_gold20_invvol_b2`, Calmar 0.712)

Both are `m31_gold20_invvol_b2` (the pin on the M31 dataset, same numbers) with `ranking.order: score` and:

| Config | vol_sessions | high_quantile | min_history_months |
|---|---|---|---|
| `m32_cvt_q80_gold20_b2` | 21 | 0.80 (the paper's quintile) | 36 |
| `m32_cvt_q75_gold20_b2` | 21 | 0.75 (the paper's quartile robustness check) | 36 |

21 sessions is one month. 36 months of history (the paper asks for 10 years; the shadow starts in 2006, leaving 4
years by 2010). No other variants.

Adoption as always: beat the pin and both baselines on Calmar, whole period and every fold. A passing config then
faces, in order: offsets 5/10/15 (win at least 3 of 4 against the gold20 offset runs), 25 bps slippage (against
`m20_gold20_slip25_b2`), and the block bootstrap (beat the pin in ≥ 80% of resamples). The 2007–09 window cannot
be checked: the shadow has only about 15 months of history by April 2007.

## Disclosure

- Known before: all results to M31; M8's conventional vol target failed; the M27 scratch script scaled the book
  monthly by its own volatility (Barroso & Santa-Clara 2015, 126-session window, every month) and saw no help in
  March 2020. Shadow-book volatility by month has not been computed or looked at for this rule.
- Expected weak spot: March 2020 started after a calm February with only its last week falling, so the end-of-February
  state may not be high; the rule then helps only from the end of March, after the drawdown's low.
