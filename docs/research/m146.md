# M146: corporate-action audit of unexplained jumps on unfactored feed rows (data quality; 2026-10-25)

Twenty-ninth cycle of the autonomous loop. No returns are used to choose any factor.

## Why

M144's value book was stopped by the gate on MANDHANA: a 2016 demerger the feed lists with no factor. The dataset
still has 993 unexplained jumps; any that are real corporate actions distort momentum scores (a demerger looks like a
65% crash, so the name drops out of the ranking for a year) and stop any book that holds through them.
`notebooks/m146_ca_audit.py` lists every unexplained jump within 3 days of a feed row of type demerger, unparsed or
other that has no override: 46 jumps on 44 names.

## What was added (`configs/data/corporate_actions_overrides.csv`, 39 rows, source `manual`, "M146" in the text)

- Splits / bonuses written in the feed's unparsed text (2005-07): exact factors from the text (CASTEXTECH 0.2,
  CYIENT 1/3, LMW 0.1, NIRMA 0.5, SUVEN 0.25, KANORICHEM 1/3). Observed ratios agree within 0.3-0.02.
- Rights with a known issue price (face value from the NSE results filings + premium): theoretical ex-rights price /
  cum close, as NCC / IDEA / RSWM (PIRGLASS 0.479, PRAXIS 0.540, NAGREEKEXP 0.550; observed 0.503 / 0.518 / 0.539).
- Demergers and schemes of arrangement (24), and rights whose issue price is unknown (8): ex-date open / cum close,
  as KPIT / TATACHEM / MANDHANA. BHAGYANGR's factor equals the m8 paper-halt override (ad31abf, never brought into
  the research branch).
- Left out: CENTEXT 2005 (rights 35:12, terms do not fit any face value) and four jumps on BIRLACOT / VISESHINFO
  (penny stocks at Rs 0.05-0.10: one-day price-tick flips around an AGM, not corporate actions; the gate still
  stops any book holding them).
