## Adoption (as M40/M47)

(a) standard bar against the reference at offset 0 (whole period, every fold, both baselines); (b) beats the
reference at 3 of 4 decision offsets; (c) passes (a) at 25 bps against the reference at 25 bps
(`m40_deliv40_gold20_slip25_b2`, run in M40); (d) capacity: p95 stock fill at most 1% of the day's turnover at a ₹10
lakh book (M40 method). If several pass, the one with the highest four-offset mean. (b)–(d) are run for all three as
information.

## Disclosure (read before any number below)

- **Selection bias.** These settings were picked by searches over 2010–2020 (C), 2010–2017 (B) and 2010–2013 (A),
  out of ~250–290 trials each. Their 2010–23 four-offset means are already known from M42: A 1.054, B 1.106, C 1.245
  (pin 0.897). So (a)–(b) are mostly a formality for C and B; the fold-level split at offset 0 is not known. The
  honest out-of-sample evidence is M42's test folds (B, C pass; A fails) and M48's 2007–09 window (all three pass).
- If one is adopted, it is a "tuned" pin. The locked 2024–26 period remains the real test, and spending it is the
  user's decision.
- Expected: C passes (a) unless one fold is weak; the risk for C is f2 (2014–17), where 45% gold cost A its test.
  Capacity is the other risk: 20 names, ₹1 crore floor, top-20% delivery.
