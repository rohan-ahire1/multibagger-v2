# M68: every near-miss in decision-day tranches (information)

Pre-registered 2026-10-02, before any M68 run.

## Why

M62 put the M61 book in four decision-day tranches (1.201 vs the pin's 1.105). Top 20 (M58) and the half blend (M59)
are the other two near-misses; they are compared here in the same form so the user's choice is not swayed by which
book was lucky on the month-end day. All sleeves already exist; only the blends are new. Nothing is adopted.
