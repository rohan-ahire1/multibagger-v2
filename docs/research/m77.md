# M77: tighter stops (7.5% and 5%)

## Question

M73 tried 10 / 15 / 20% stops; 10% was best and was the tightest tried, so the optimum may lie below it. Han, Zhou &
Zhu (2016) report results for stops from 5% up. Grid extension:

| Candidate | Change from the pin |
|---|---|
| `m77_stop75_b2` | 7.5% stop from the rebalance open (as M72/M73) |
| `m77_stop5_b2` | 5% stop |

Two variants. Known before: 10% (1.346; tranches 1.513), 15% (1.239), 20% (1.176). Expected: more whipsaw and costs
at 5%. Same checks and bar as M73–M76 (offsets 3 of 4, 25 bps, 2007–09 mean ≥ 0.284, tranches above the pin's).

**Verdict: both fail** (f1 and f2 at offset 0; 7.5% also fails offsets 10 and 15).

Information: tighter stops trade return for drawdown almost one for one. In tranches 7.5% has the best Calmar so far
(1.551) but 4.7 points less CAGR than the pin; 5% gives up 8 points. Results swing widely with the decision day
(7.5%: 2.10 at offset 5, 1.02 at offset 10), so a single-day stop book is a timing bet; tranches are the honest form.
The Calmar peak is flat between 7.5% and 10%.
