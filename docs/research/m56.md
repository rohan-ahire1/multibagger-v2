# M56: cut the new pin's trading (hold buffer, no resizing)

Pre-registered 2026-10-02, before any run.

## Idea

The pin `m49_b_b2` turns its book over 6.7 times a year (run 20261002-000607-0103cc7f). Novy-Marx & Velikov (2016,
RFS, "A taxonomy of anomalies and their trading costs") show a hold buffer ("banding": buy at rank ≤ N, sell only
below a wider rank) keeps most of momentum's gross return at far lower cost. M5/M8 tried this on older books, which
had no delivery filter and other settings; this pin trades a smaller, different universe, so it is checked again.

## Runs (question `momentum-m56`, against `momentum/m49_b_b2`)

| Config | Change from the pin |
|---|---|
| `m56_hold35_b2` | `hold_rank_max: 35` |
| `m56_hold50_b2` | `hold_rank_max: 50` |
| `m56_noresize_b2` | `resize_held: false` (kept names are not traded back to target weight) |

Bar as M49 (whole period and every fold; then 3 of 4 offsets, 25 bps, capacity). No other variants.
