# M103: a 1% band on the Next 50 filter

## Question

M95's filter decides once a month whether JUNIORBEES is above its 200-day average. Near the line it can flip back and
forth (2022: off at the end of February, back on at the end of March, off again in May while the book fell). Brock,
Lakonishok & LeBaron (1992) add a 1% band to moving-average rules: switch off only when the price is more than 1%
below the average, back on only when more than 1% above; in between keep the last state. On month-end dates
2010–23 this changes six months (2013-06, 2015-11, 2018-07, 2019-04, 2022-03, 2022-07; checked before
pre-registration, disclosed).

New code: `regime.band_frac` (`ParamsM103`); refused by paper.

| Candidate | Change |
|---|---|
| `m103_band1_r50g_jr*` | `m95_r50g_jr*` with `band_frac: 0.01` |

One variant (BLL's 1%). Same checks and bar. Disclosure: M95–M102 seen first.

**Verdict:** the tranches still pass, but the band makes the book slightly worse on the whole period, fold 1 and
2007–09; offset 0 now fails fold 1. The whipsaw months it removes did not cost much, and the delay costs about as
much elsewhere. M95 (no band) stays the lead.
