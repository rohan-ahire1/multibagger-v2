# M40: delivery share as a filter on the winners

Pre-registration (2026-10-01, Claude), written and committed before any run of these configs and before the delivery
data has been looked at by stock. Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`. Dataset
`nse_research@2026-10-01c` (01b plus the NSE delivery-position files; prices unchanged).

## Why

Every NSE bhavcopy session also has a security-wise delivery file (`MTO_DDMMYYYY.DAT`): the quantity traded and the
quantity that went to delivery, the rest being squared off the same day. Indian practitioners read a high delivery
share as buying by holders and a low one as intraday speculation. In academic terms, momentum driven by
attention and short-horizon trading is the kind that reverses (Lee & Swaminathan 2000: high-volume winners mean-revert
sooner; Da, Gurun & Warachka 2014 and Barber & Odean 2008 on attention). A low delivery share over the formation
window is a direct, India-specific measure of that speculative turnover. No academic study of delivery share and
momentum in India was found, so this is a new test, not a replication.

## Data

`mb data fetch delivery` downloads one file a session (2005 onwards) to `data/raw/nse_delivery/<year>/<date>.DAT`.
The build joins `deliv_qty` to the bars on (date, symbol, series) before rename linking. Bars with no delivery record
keep a null. Prices, adjustments and checks are unchanged, so the pin must re-run identically on 01c (checked by
`m40_base_gold20_b2` below, which must equal the pin's numbers exactly).

## The rule (`UniverseM40.delivery_drop_frac`, config model `ParamsM40`)

At each decision date, for every otherwise eligible name: delivery share = sum of `deliv_qty` / sum of `volume`, over
the sessions in the momentum formation span (from `lookback_sessions` to `skip_sessions` ago, 231 sessions) that
have a delivery record. A name with no record in that span is not eligible. The `delivery_drop_frac` share (rounded
up) of eligible names with the lowest share is dropped; ranking and everything else are unchanged.

## Runs (question `momentum-m40-delivery`, against the pin `momentum/m33_adv2cr_gold20_b2`, Calmar 0.865)

All are the pin on `nse_research@2026-10-01c`, written with the full `ParamsM40` keys (all new options off), and:

| Config | delivery_drop_frac |
|---|---|
| `m40_base_gold20_b2` | null (control: must equal the pin; not a variant) |
| `m40_deliv20_gold20_b2` | 0.20 (bottom quintile): **the candidate** |
| `m40_deliv40_gold20_b2` | 0.40 (second variant) |

No other variants. Adoption as always: beat the pin and both baselines on Calmar, whole period and every fold. A
passing config then faces, in order: offsets 5/10/15 (win at least 3 of 4 against `m33_adv2cr_gold20_off{5,10,15}_b2`),
25 bps slippage (against `m33_adv2cr_gold20_slip25_b2`), the block bootstrap (beat the pin in ≥ 80% of resamples),
and, as information, the 2007-04..2009-12 window against `m35_early_adv2cr_gold20_b2`.

## Disclosure

- Known before: all results to M39. Before writing this, only file format and coverage were checked (a handful of
  2005–2026 sample files parse the same way). Delivery shares of individual stocks, and any link between delivery
  share and returns in this data, have not been computed or looked at.
- Expected weak spots: the filter shrinks the pool by a fifth, so at the ₹2 crore floor the book may move into
  less liquid names; and a high delivery share is also typical of quiet, low-turnover stocks, which may not be the
  winners that matter.
