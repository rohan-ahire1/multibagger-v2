# M67: a lookback ensemble (189 / 273 / 378 sessions, a third each)

Pre-registered 2026-10-02, before any M67 run.

## Why

The pin's lookback (273) is its most sensitive setting (M50: 252 → 1.082, 294 → 1.053), and M42's three walk-forward
steps picked 378, 273 and 189. Rather than pick one, hold a third of the book on each (Newfound Research, "Diversifying
specification risk", Hoffstein 2018–20; the same logic as decision-day tranches in M54/M62). The sleeves differ from
the pin only in lookback. Expectation: a smoother book with about the pin's average return; fold 2 is the risk, since
both other lookbacks gave up fold 2 inside their own tuned configs (M49).
