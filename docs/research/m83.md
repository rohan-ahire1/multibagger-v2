# M83: M21's book stop on the current pin

## Question

M82's fold table: the 10% name stop never lowers the 2010–17 drawdown; it only costs 2.6–3.2 points of CAGR a year
there, and all its benefit is the 2020 crash. A crash shows up as a fall of the whole book, so a stop on the book
should fire less often in calm years. M21 built exactly that (`exits.book_stop_frac`: the picks, followed from the
decision close at their target weights, gold left out, fall to (1 − f) × their high since the decision → sold at the
next open, cash until the next decision). It failed on the old pin (`m19_gold20_invvol_b2`, ₹5 crore floor, no
delivery filter): most stops were whipsaws (2016-01, 2016-11, 2022), and the worst drawdown moved to a slow 2010–11
bear. The current pin is a different book (top 25, ₹2 crore floor, delivery filter, gold 0.25) and the checks are
wider now (offsets, tranches, 2007–09), so M21's rule is re-tested unchanged, as M72 did with other old overlays. No
new code.

| Candidate | Change from the pin |
|---|---|
| `m83_bstop10_b2` | `book_stop_frac: 0.10` (M21's number) |
| `m83_bstop15_b2` | `book_stop_frac: 0.15` (M21's number) |

Two variants. Known before: M21's failure, M72–M82 (the name stop helps only in 2020). My expectation from M21 is a
fail in f1/f2. Same checks and bar as M73–M82 (main bar at offset 0; offsets 3 of 4; 25 bps; 2007–09 mean ≥ 0.284;
tranches above the pin's tranches 1.105; 1.590 / 2.199 / 1.312).
