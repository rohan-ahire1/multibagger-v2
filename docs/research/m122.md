# M122: market state (Next 50's 24- or 36-month return) as a second risk-off condition (pre-registered 2026-10-23)

Third cycle of the autonomous loop. Written before any M122 backtest.

## Idea and sources

Momentum pays after up markets and loses after down markets: Cooper, Gutierrez & Hameed (2004, *Journal of
Finance*) define the state by the market's 36-month return (US 1929–95: 0.93% a month after up states, −0.37% after
down states); international follow-ups find the same in emerging markets (momentum larger in up markets). Daniel &
Moskowitz (2016, *JFE*) use the market's 24-month return as the bear-market indicator in which momentum crashes. M106
already goes half to gold when JUNIORBEES is below its 200-day average, a short-horizon trend rule. This adds the
slow, published state on the same index. Regime variants tried before (breadth M99, graded M97, band M103, F&O signals
M118) used other signals, not the market's own multi-year return.

## Rule

At a decision d, risk-off (the usual M106 risk-off: half the stock book to GOLDBEES) when JUNIORBEES < its 200-day
average (unchanged) OR JUNIORBEES's adj_close at d is below its adj_close `state_sessions` sessions earlier.
Code: `RegimeM122`/`ParamsM122` (`params.py`), `market_state_up` (`targets.py`), `tests/test_m122_market_state.py`.

rebound months, where risk-off is likely to cost.

## The bar (LOOP.md, unchanged)

Against `m120_ref*`: (1) whole-period Calmar higher before and after tax at offset 0, and before tax at offsets
5/10/15; (2) no fold lost (offset 0, before tax); (3) higher at 25 bps; (4) neighbours (st36: 630/882; st24: 378/630
sessions) do not collapse, bootstrap ≥ 85%. If both pass, the higher four-offset mean wins. No `--final` run.

Fail: any point above. Expected: weak; the extra risk-off months mostly fall in recoveries (2012, 2020), where
momentum did well in India. Variants tried before M122: about 994; M122 adds 2.
