# M86: the stop book without gold

## Question

The pin holds 25% gold as crash insurance. With the 10% stop doing that job (M72–M85), the gold may only cost return:
M74 cut it to 10% and the tranche CAGR rose from 26.9% to 30.5% (drawdown 17.8% → 20.1%). The user asked for a higher
CAGR as well as Calmar ≥ 1. The natural endpoint is no gold at all (0 is not tuned).

| Candidate | Change from the pin |
|---|---|
| `m86_g0s10_b2` | no gold (`fixed_weights: {}`), 10% stop |
| `m86_half_g0s10_b2` (blend) | 50/50 `m86_g0s10` and M78's stopped rotation `m78_rot_s10` |

Two variants. Known before: M74 (g10s10), M78 (half_g10s10 tranches 28.9% CAGR, 18.9% DD), M85. Same checks and bar
as M73–M85 (main bar at offset 0; offsets 3 of 4; 25 bps; 2007–09 mean ≥ 0.284; tranches above the pin's tranches
1.105; 1.590 / 2.199 / 1.312). Expected: higher CAGR, deeper drawdown, a fail on f2.

Information: dropping gold buys CAGR at about the same Calmar. g0s10 in tranches is the highest-CAGR book with
Calmar ≥ 1.5 found (32.8% vs the pin tranches' 29.5%, drawdown 21.7% vs 26.7%), but with no gold it loses 55% in
2008: a monthly-reset stop does not stop a slow bear. The half form keeps 30.3% CAGR and halves the 2008 damage
(0.561). Gold's role is the slow bear, the stop's role is the fast crash.
