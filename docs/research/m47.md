# M47: a stronger delivery filter

Pre-registration (2026-10-02, Claude), committed before any M47 run. Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31; no
`--final`. Dataset `nse_research@2026-10-02` (M46). Reference: `momentum/m46_ref_d1002_b2`, the pin's settings on this
dataset (identical numbers to the pin: Calmar 0.913, four-offset mean 0.8975).

## Why

M40 dropped the eligible names with the lowest delivery share (Lee & Swaminathan 2000: high-turnover, speculative
winners reverse); 20% helped and 40% helped more. In M42 every walk-forward step, each tuning only on its own past,
chose the strongest filter allowed (drop 80%), while the other settings disagreed. Tested here alone, changing nothing
else, as an ordinary candidate.

## Configs

| Config | Change from the pin |
|---|---|
| `m47_deliv60_gold20_b2` | `delivery_drop_frac: 0.6` |
| `m47_deliv80_gold20_b2` | `delivery_drop_frac: 0.8` |

Two round values; 0.8 is M42's pick. Question `momentum-m47` (2 variants).

## Adoption (as M40)

A candidate must: (a) pass the standard bar against the reference (whole period, every fold, both baselines);
(b) beat the reference at 3 of 4 decision offsets (0/5/10/15), on the same dataset; (c) pass at 25 bps;
(d) capacity: p95 fill at most 1% of the day's turnover at a ₹10 lakh book (`notebooks/m40_capacity.py` method).
Information only: the 2007-04..2009-12 window at the four offsets against the reference's settings there. If both
pass, the one with the higher four-offset mean is taken. Checks (b)–(d) run only for configs that pass (a).

## Disclosure

- Known before: all to M46, in particular M41/M42's finding that 0.6–0.8 was chosen in every training window, and that
  the 2010–23 in-sample scores of those full settings were 1.05–1.25. That is why this test is weaker evidence than M40:
  the value was picked by searches that saw parts of the same period. The step test folds (2014–17, 2018–23, 2021–23)
  and this run's folds overlap. The 2007–09 window and the locked 2024–26 period are the only data no search saw.
- Expected: higher Calmar in f3 and the whole period, smaller pool and higher concentration in thinner names (capacity
  is the likely failure point), f2 uncertain.
