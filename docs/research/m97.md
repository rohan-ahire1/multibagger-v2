# M97: a graded filter on both Nifty 50 and Nifty Next 50

## Question

M89's filter watches Nifty 50 (NIFTYBEES) and fails fold 1; M95's watches Nifty Next 50 (JUNIORBEES) and passes in
tranches, but its offset-0 fold 2 and whole-period drawdown are worse than M89's. Each catches slides the other
misses (2011 vs 2013). Averaging the two signals is the standard way to use both without choosing (signal
diversification): with a fraction f of the benchmarks above their 200-day averages, the book holds f + (1 − f) × 0.5
in stocks and the rest in gold. So both above: all stocks; one below: a quarter to gold; both below: half to gold.

New code: `regime.extra_benchmark_symbols` (`ParamsM97`); with an empty list a run is unchanged (test).

| Candidate | Change |
|---|---|
| `m97_r50g_nj_b2` | `m89_g0s10_r50g_b2` + `extra_benchmark_symbols: [JUNIORBEES]` |

One variant. Same checks and bar: offsets, 25 bps, 2007–09, tranches (vs the pin's tranches and the pinned run).
Disclosure: M89 and M95 results were seen first; the 0.25 step is implied by r50g, not chosen.
