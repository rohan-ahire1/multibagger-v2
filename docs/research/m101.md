# M101: half top 10, half top 20 on the Next 50 filtered rule

## Question

M98 at the end of fold 1 is similar). A fixed half-and-half mix of the two tranche books (eight sleeves at 1/8, never
rebalanced, as every blend here) spreads the money over about 20–25 names. Does the mix beat both?
