# M123: a short-term reversal guard at entry (pre-registered 2026-10-23, before any M123 backtest)

Fourth cycle of the autonomous loop.

## Idea and sources

Last month's winners tend to give some of it back next month (Jegadeesh 1990; Lehmann 1990). In India, large
one-month price moves in large liquid stocks are followed by reversals, stronger after bigger moves (Economic Thought
2022, "On stock return patterns following large monthly price movements: empirical evidence from India", 2000–2019),
and short-term reversals show up among illiquid BSE stocks (Chui, Ranganathan, Rohit & Veeraraghavan 2023, *PBFJ*).
M106 skips the last 14 sessions when it ranks, but it still buys a name right after a spike. The guard waits.
M72's MAX filter used the single largest day; M109 nudged the skip (11/17 sessions); neither refused recent spikes.

## Rule

Short-term return at decision d: adj_close at d / adj_close 21 sessions earlier − 1. Among the ranked names that have it,
the top `drop_frac` (ties by symbol) are not bought if not already held (`nosell`, M111's path); held names untouched.
Code: `ShortReversal`/`ParamsM123`, `top_returns` (shared with M121), tests in `tests/test_m121_long_reversal.py`.

## Candidates (M106 + one change; dev 2010-01-04..2023-12-31, `nse_research@2026-10-14`)

- `m123_sr10`: drop_frac 0.10; `m123_sr20`: drop_frac 0.20. Offsets 0/5/10/15 and 25 bps; reference `m120_ref*`.

Information (`notebooks/m123_reversal.py`, no returns after the buy): 21.5% of M106's 1,278 new buys are in the top 10%
of last-month returns (275) and 34.4% in the top 20% (440): the rule binds about twice the base rate, since momentum
names often had a strong last month. A refused name can be bought a month later if it is still ranked.

## The bar (LOOP.md, unchanged)

Against `m120_ref*`: (1) Calmar higher before and after tax at offset 0, before tax at offsets 5/10/15; (2) no fold
lost; (3) higher at 25 bps; (4) neighbours (drop_frac 0.05/0.15; sessions 10/42) hold, bootstrap ≥ 85%. If both pass,
the higher four-offset mean wins. No `--final` run.

Fail: any point above. Expected: uncertain; delaying buys of strong names may cut return in trending markets.
