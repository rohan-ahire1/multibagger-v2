# M48: M42's tuned settings on 2007–09, the window no search saw

Pre-registration (2026-10-02, Claude), committed before any M48 run. Window 2007-04-02..2009-12-31 (the M35 early
window, before the development period); dataset `nse_research@2026-10-02b` (the GEOINFO fix lets offset 15 run). No
`--final`; the locked period is not touched.

## Why

M42's three walk-forward steps each picked settings on their own past (all training windows start 2010-01-04); B and
C beat the pin on their test folds, A did not. Their test folds overlap the period the pin was chosen on, so the
cleanest data left for them is 2007–09 (including the 2008 crash), which no search and no tuning saw. It is short and
holds one regime, so this is evidence, not an adoption test.

## What is read

For each set, the four-offset mean Calmar, CAGR and max DD on 2007-04..2009-12. A tuned setting "holds up" if its
four-offset mean Calmar beats the pin's. If B and C both hold up, a stronger case exists for proposing a tuned
setting (still subject to the user's decision on the locked period); if neither does, the M42 gains are treated as fit
to 2010–23.

## Disclosure

Known before: M40's early numbers for the pin's settings at offsets 0/5/10 (Calmar 0.19–0.22, 58% DD); the general
2008 picture (every book lost ~58–65%). Expected: the high-gold sets (A, C) lose less in 2008 and so score higher; B
is closest to the pin.
