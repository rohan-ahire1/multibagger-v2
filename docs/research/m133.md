# M133: the price floor, ₹25 / ₹100 instead of ₹50 (pre-registered 2026-10-23, before any M133 backtest)

Fifteenth cycle of the autonomous loop.

## Why

M106 drops stocks closing below ₹50 (raw close, decision date). The value came from v1 and has never been varied: the
M50 neighbour sweep covered lookback, skip, top N, gold, the volatility window and the floors on turnover, not the
price floor. So this is both a missing robustness check (LOOP bar point 4 for M106 itself) and a candidate. Low
nominal prices go with lottery-like trading and retail speculation (Birru & Wang 2016, *JFE*, "Nominal price
illusion": investors overestimate the skewness of low-priced stocks; Indian practice: "no stocks under ₹50/100");
a higher floor removes more of them, a lower one admits more momentum winners from the cheap end.

## Candidates (M106 + one change; dev 2010-01-04..2023-12-31, `nse_research@2026-10-14`)

- `m133_p25` (₹25) and `m133_p100` (₹100). Offsets 0/5/10/15 and 25 bps; reference `m120_ref*`. Pre-screen: four
  offsets first. If both pass, the higher four-offset mean wins; ₹50 and the other are its neighbours.

## The bar (LOOP.md, unchanged)

Against `m120_ref*`: (1) Calmar higher before and after tax at offset 0, before tax at offsets 5/10/15; (2) no fold
lost; (3) higher at 25 bps; (4) neighbours hold, bootstrap ≥ 85%. No `--final` run.

Fail: any point above. Information either way: if both are close to M106, the floor is not a knife edge. Variants
tried before M133: about 1,010; M133 adds 2.
