# M62: the M61 book in four decision-day tranches (information for a live choice)

Pre-registered 2026-10-02, before any M62 run.

## Why

M59 and M61 depend on the decision day (offset 5 is weak). A live book would avoid that by splitting the capital over
the four decision days (Hoffstein, Faber & Braun 2020; as M28, M36, M54). This measures that form: 12.5% each of
`m58_top20{,_off5,_off10,_off15}_b2` and `m61_rot_top20{,_off5,_off10,_off15}_b2`, never rebalanced against each other.
Compared with the pin's own tranche form `m54_b_tranche_b2` (1.105, run 20261002-001643-d6a3c453), and on 2007–09 with
the same split of `m58_early_top20*` / `m61_early_rot_top20*` against the pin's four early runs.
