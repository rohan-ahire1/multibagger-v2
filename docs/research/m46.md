# M46: rebalance every three months

Pre-registration (2026-10-02, Claude), committed before the code is used for any run. Development period
2010-01-04..2023-12-31; no `--final`. Dataset `nse_research@2026-10-01c`. Base: the pin `momentum/m40_deliv40_gold20_b2`
(Calmar 0.913; four-offset mean 0.8975).

## Why

Every book so far trades once a month; holding period was never tested. Jegadeesh & Titman (1993, Table 1) find the
12-month formation profitable at 3-, 6-, 9- and 12-month holding periods, and Novy-Marx & Velikov (2016, *A Taxonomy of
Anomalies and Their Trading Costs*, RFS) show that momentum's net return is eaten mostly by turnover, which a longer
hold cuts. A 12-1 rank moves slowly, so most of a month's trades only resize names already held. The pin pays about
50 bps a side in a ₹2 crore-floor universe; trading a third as often could keep more of the return.

## The option (`ScheduleM46.every_months`, new models `ScheduleM46` / `ParamsM46`)

Decide only at month-ends whose month number satisfies `month % every_months == phase_month`; skip the rest. Nothing
else changes: the same rank, delivery filter, inverse-vol weights and 20% gold, which is now also rebalanced only on
decision dates. `every_months: 1, phase_month: 0` must equal the pin exactly (control). Paper trading refuses
`every_months > 1`.

| `m46_q3p0_slip25_*` | `momentum-m46-checks` | phase 0 at 25 bps slippage |

3 months is the paper's shortest tested hold; not tuned. No 2- or 6-month runs.

## Adoption

The candidate must (a) pass the standard bar against the pin (whole period and every fold, both baselines);
(b) its mean Calmar over all 12 phase × offset runs must beat the pin's four-offset mean (0.8975), since a quarterly
book has 3× the timing luck of a monthly one; (c) pass at 25 bps. If (a) fails, the check runs are still made, because
(b) is the more honest number, but nothing is adopted.

## Expected

Known before: everything to M45. Expected: turnover roughly a third, CAGR about the same, drawdown unchanged or worse
(March 2020 falls between two phase-0 decisions; a staler book reacts later). A fail on (a) is likely; the spread across
phases is the main thing learned.
