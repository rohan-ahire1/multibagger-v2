# M38: the ₹2 crore floor on the early window, over all four decision days

Pre-registration (2026-10-01, Claude), committed before these runs.

## Why

M35 tested the ₹2 crore floor (the pin's only adopted gain since M19) on 2007–09 at the month-end decision day only
and found it worse than the ₹5 crore floor (Calmar 0.109 vs 0.150). M37 then showed the decision day alone moves
2007–09 CAGR between 7% and 15%, so a one-day comparison is mostly noise. This repeats M35 at offsets 5, 10 and 15.

## Runs (question `momentum-m38-early-floor`, information only)

₹2 crore: `m35_early_adv2cr_gold20_b2` and `m37_early_adv2cr_gold20_off{5,10,15}_b2` (already run in M35/M37).
₹5 crore: `m35_early_gold20_invvol_b2` (run) and new `m38_early_gold20_invvol_off{5,10,15}_b2`.

Read-out, fixed now: the floor is "confirmed early" if ₹2 crore has the higher CAGR at 3 or more of the 4 offsets
and its mean CAGR over the four is higher; "rejected early" if ₹5 crore wins 3 or more; otherwise "unclear". Nothing
is adopted or dropped on this alone; it sets how much of the M33 gain to expect live.
