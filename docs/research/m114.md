# M114: the last results reaction as a filter on the momentum picks (pre-registered 2026-10-04)

Asked by the user ("keep improving"). Research elsewhere finds price momentum works best where the latest earnings
news was good: the stock's return around its last results date (earnings announcement return) predicts the next
months, and momentum stocks whose last results were badly received tend to stall. M106 does not use results dates.

## Data

NSE board-meeting announcements (`data/raw/nse_board_meetings/`, copied from india-market-data
`data/nse_board_meetings`, meetings 2008-01 to 2026-10, ~4 results meetings per symbol per year, announced a median 9
days ahead). A meeting counts as a results meeting if its purpose mentions "result" or its description mentions
"financial result" (any case). One meeting per symbol and date.

Reaction to a results meeting on date m: the stock's adjusted close return from the last session before m to the
first session after m (results come out during or after the meeting day), minus NIFTYBEES's return over the same two
closes. It is usable at a decision d (decided after d's close) if that first session after m is on or before d.
Signal at d: the reaction of the latest usable results meeting held within 100 calendar days before d; no such meeting,
or a missing close in the window, means no signal.

## Candidates (written before any M114 run; M106 + one change each)

- `m114_noneg`: a name not already held is not bought if its signal is below 0.
- `m114_posfirst`: new buys first take names with signal above 0 ranked under 50 (in rank order), then the rest in
  rank order.
Same code path as M111's insider rules (only the signal differs), so held names and weights are untouched.
