# M118: options and futures market signals as a second risk-off switch on M106 (pre-registered 2026-10-14)

Asked by the user ("try as many different things as possible", using the market data we now have; idea 1 of the
follow-ups: F&O signals).

## Why market-level, not per-stock

The first plan was to filter M106's buys with per-stock option signals (call-put IV spread, skew, option volume).
Measured first, for information only (no returns used to choose anything): only 8.6% of M106's 1,271 dev buys
(run 20261002-050950-93574646) were stocks with listed options on the session before entry, and about 1% from 2014
on (2021: 0 of 110). The picks are small caps. A per-stock options filter would almost never bind, so it is dropped.
Market-level signals act on the whole book instead.

## Data

Copied from india-market-data into `data/raw/` (immutable):
- `nse_india_vix`: India VIX daily close, 2010-07-19..2026-10-01.
- `nse_fo_bhav`: NSE F&O bhavcopy 2009-2026 (old format to mid-2024, UDiFF after; volume is contracts in both).
  Used here: NIFTY index options (`OPTIDX`), open interest by put/call. Present on every CM session from 2009-06 to
  2026-10-01 except 2013-10-09 and 2021-03-30.
- `nse_participant_oi`: open interest by participant type, 2012-01-02..2026-10-01; missing 2013-10-09, 2016-02-10,
  2025-09-19. Used: the FII row, index futures long and short contracts.

A value counts from the day it is known: VIX on its own date (an index close, like the prices M106 ranks on);
the F&O bhavcopy and participant OI from the next day (NSE publishes them in the evening). At a decision d the signal
uses the latest value usable at d; one older than 7 calendar days is an error.

## Candidates (M106 + one change each)

Each adds a second risk-off condition. A decision is risk-off (half the stock share to GOLDBEES, as today) when
JUNIORBEES is below its 200-day average **or** the signal says off. Nothing else changes. Thresholds are fixed here,
before any run, from the cited work or a plain round choice; none is tuned.

1. `m118_vrp`, variance risk premium (Bollerslev, Tauchen & Zhou 2009: a low premium predicts low index returns over
   the next months). VRP = (VIX/100)^2 / 12 - the sum of squared daily log returns of NIFTYBEES (adj_close) over the
   21 sessions to d. Off when VRP < 0, that is when the market has recently moved more than options price for the next
   month. Off for decisions before VIX data (January-June 2010): the signal is treated as on there.
2. `m118_pcr`, NIFTY put-call ratio on open interest (sentiment; heavy put open interest has been read as hedged or
   bullish positioning, a low ratio as complacency). PCR = total put OI / total call OI over all NIFTY option expiries.
   Off when the latest PCR ranks in the bottom 20% of its last 250 values (fraction of those values strictly below it
   < 0.20). A rank, not a level, because the ratio's level drifts (median 1.29 in 2010, 0.92 in 2025).
3. `m118_fii`, FII index futures positioning (foreign flows lead emerging-market returns: Froot, O'Connell & Seasholes
   2001). Long share = FII index futures long / (long + short). Off when it ranks in the bottom 20% of its last 250
   values. A rank again: the level fell from ~0.7 (2014) to ~0.15 (2025), largely because FIIs hedge cash holdings.

For the ranked signals, decisions before a full 250-value window (PCR: none in dev; FII: decisions to about
January 2013) treat the signal as on.

Information, before the backtests (signal data only, no returns): for each candidate, the number of dev decisions it
turns risk-off where JUNIORBEES alone is risk-on, by year.
