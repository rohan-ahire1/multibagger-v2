# M71: a smaller rotation share in the pin/rotation blend

## Question

M59's 50/50 blend of the pin (`m49_b_b2`) and its gold rotation (`m59_rot_b2`) failed only fold 2 (1.825 vs 2.072):
the rotation sleeve's own fold-2 drawdown (20.7%) is deeper than the pin's (13.4%). Does a smaller rotation share keep
fold 2 while keeping part of the crash protection?
