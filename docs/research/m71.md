# M71: a smaller rotation share in the pin/rotation blend

## Question

M59's 50/50 blend of the pin (`m49_b_b2`) and its gold rotation (`m59_rot_b2`) failed only fold 2 (1.825 vs 2.072):
the rotation sleeve's own fold-2 drawdown (20.7%) is deeper than the pin's (13.4%). Does a smaller rotation share keep
fold 2 while keeping part of the crash protection?

## Candidates and checks

`blend/m71_w80_b2` (0.8 pin / 0.2 rotation) and `blend/m71_w90_b2` (0.9 / 0.1), never rebalanced, sleeves pinned by
hash. Each must also pass, as the pin did:

1. 3 of 4 decision offsets beat the pin's (1.125 / 1.101 / 1.055 / 1.142) — blends of the existing offset sleeves.
2. 25 bps a side beats `m49_b_slip25_b2` (1.078; folds 1.498 / 1.971 / 1.310) on the whole period and every fold.
3. 2007–09 four-offset mean not below the pin's 0.284.
4. Capacity p95 ≤ 1% (both sleeves already pass on their own).

If both pass, 0.8 is preferred (more crash protection). Expected: likely a pass on 1 and 3, a coin flip on 2's fold 2.
