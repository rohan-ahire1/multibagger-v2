# M36: the M27/M28 gold rotation on the ₹2 crore book

Pre-registration (2026-10-01, Claude), written and committed before any of these configs is run. Development period
2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`. Dataset `nse_research@2026-10-01b`. Code includes the M35 fund-unit fix.

## Why

M35 showed that every stock-momentum book, with or without 20% gold, lost about 65% in 2008, and that the only rule
tried that defends a 2008-type crash is the M27 book-or-gold rotation (2007–09: 41% max DD, Calmar 0.824 with the
fix). M27/M28 rejected the rotation on the ₹5 crore book because it lost folds 1 and 2 and its edge depended on the
decision day; in tranches (M28) it cut the worst loss from 31% to 22% for about 2 points of CAGR a year. M33 then
found the ₹2 crore floor raises the book's return in every fold. This milestone asks one question: does the M28
design, unchanged, pass on the new book? No parameter is new or tuned: the rotation (GOLDBEES, Keller's 21/63/126/252
lookbacks, top 1, no cash floor), the four offsets and the 25% tranche weights are M27/M28's.

## Runs (question `momentum-m36-rot-floor`, against the pin `momentum/m33_adv2cr_gold20_b2`, Calmar 0.865)

| `m36_rot_tranche_b2` (blend) | 25% each of the four rotation runs: **the candidate** |
| `m36_gold20_tranche_b2` (blend) | 25% each of the pin and its offset runs `m33_adv2cr_gold20_off{5,10,15}_b2`: the timing-free reference |
| `m36_half_tranche_b2` (blend) | 12.5% each of the eight sleeves above |

Adoption as always: beat the pin and both baselines on Calmar, whole period and each fold. The candidate is
`m36_rot_tranche_b2`; `m36_half_tranche_b2` is a second candidate (two variants under the question). As in M28, a
candidate must also beat `m36_gold20_tranche_b2` in every fold, so the gain is not just the removal of timing luck. A
passing candidate then faces 25 bps slippage (all its sleeves on `nse_cash_slip25` vs the pin's 25 bps run), the block
bootstrap (beat the pin in ≥ 80% of resamples) and capacity. The offset-0 sleeve alone is information only.

Information only, not part of the bar: `m36_early_rot_adv2cr_b2` (the offset-0 sleeve on 2007-04-02..2009-12-31,
question `momentum-m36-info`), against `m35_early_adv2cr_gold20_b2` (Calmar 0.109, 65% DD).

  f1 0.376, f2 1.255 vs gold20 tranches 0.710, f1 0.444, f2 1.558), the ₹2 crore pin's folds (0.662 / 1.788 / 0.939),
  and the M35 early-window rotation (0.824). No rotation has been run with the ₹2 crore floor.
- Expected: fails fold 2 (2014–17 was a long bull market for small and mid caps; any move to gold costs return there),
  as it did on the ₹5 crore book. It is run anyway because it is the only structure with a known crash defence, and
  its cost in calm years on the better book is worth measuring.

### Bootstrap (information only; `notebooks/m36_bootstrap.py`, the M20 settings)

A = half tranche, B = pin, C = gold20 tranche. A beats B on Calmar in 44.8% of resamples (CAGR higher in 6.3%), and C
in 64.8%. Median Calmar A 0.867, B 0.875, C 0.810. So the half tranche's 0.951 is not a robust gain over the pin; it
buys a smaller worst loss with about 2.4 points of CAGR a year.

Side finding: C (the pin's own four decision days) has a lower Calmar than B (month-end only) in **96.9%** of
resamples (diff 5–95%: −0.155 to −0.009). Deciding on the last session of the month is consistently better than 5–15
sessions earlier for this book, not just luckier on a few dates. Not studied further here; a turn-of-month effect
in Indian stocks is documented elsewhere and would be the thing to pre-register if this is followed up.

### Verdict

Not adopted; the pin stays. For a live book the choice is a preference, not a research result: the half tranche
gives up about 2.4 points of CAGR a year for a 26% instead of 31% worst loss in 2010–2023, and, at offset 0, a 38%
instead of 65% loss in 2007–09. That trade-off is for the user to decide.
