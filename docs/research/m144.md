# M144: a value sleeve beside M106 (pre-registered 2026-10-25, before any M144 backtest)

Twenty-seventh cycle of the autonomous loop.

## Why

Every overlay on M106 (filters, ranks, weights, regime, stops) has failed. The one structural gain seen is a
diversifying sleeve: M142's pullback blend wins whole-period Calmar everywhere but loses f2. Value is the classic
diversifier for momentum: Asness, Moskowitz & Pedersen (2013, JF, "Value and momentum everywhere") find value and
momentum negatively correlated in every market and asset class, so a 50/50 mix has a higher Sharpe than either.
India: NSE runs Nifty500 Value 50 (earnings yield) and multi-factor MQVLv indices. The low-vol sleeve (M29) is the
only stock sleeve tried before, and it failed f3. A value book could not be built before M141 because there was no
earnings or share data.

## The value book (`strategies/value/configs/m144_value.yaml`, new strategy kind `value`)

At each month's last session: EQ names, raw close ≥ ₹50, 50-session mean turnover ≥ ₹1 crore (M106's floors), no
fund units. Earnings yield = trailing four quarters' standalone net profit (first filings known at the decision,
latest quarter ended within 200 days) / market cap (M143's carried share count x close). Positive earnings only.
Top 25, equal weight, traded at the next open, fully invested, no regime filter, no gold. Nothing tuned: the
settings are M106's floors and a 25-name book. Code: `src/mb/strategy/value/`, `fundamentals.ttm_profit` /
`market_caps`. Tests: `tests/test_m144_value.py`.

## Candidates (dev 2010-01-04..2023-12-31, `nse_research@2026-10-23`)

Blends rebalanced monthly to fixed weights, as M142 (`strategies/blend/configs/`): `m144_v20` (M106 0.8 + value 0.2)
and `m144_v30` (0.7 / 0.3), each with the momentum sleeve at offsets 0/5/10/15 (the value sleeve always decides at
month-end), and at 25 bps (`_slip25`, both sleeves at 25 bps). The value book alone is reported as information.

## The bar (LOOP.md, unchanged)

Against M106: (1) Calmar higher before and after tax at offset 0 (`notebooks/m142_tax.py`) and before tax at offsets
5/10/15; (2) no fold lost at offset 0; (3) higher at 25 bps; (4) the other weight as the neighbour holds, block
bootstrap ≥ 85%. Pre-screen first. No `--final`.

Prior: on 2010-23 NSE data, value lagged badly from 2014 to 2020, and M106 makes 30% a year, so a value sleeve will
probably cost more CAGR than it saves in drawdown. Expected fail. Variants before M144: about 1,029; M144 adds 2.
