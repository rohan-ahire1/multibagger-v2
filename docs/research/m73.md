# M73: stop-loss levels on the current pin

## Question

M72's 10% stop (Han, Zhou & Zhu 2016) cut the pin's max DD from 26.1% to 20.1% (Calmar 1.346) but failed folds 1 and 2:
it sells winners on ordinary dips in bull years. Does a looser stop keep the crash cut and lose less in those years?

Candidates: `m73_stop15_b2`, `m73_stop20_b2` (the pin with `stop_loss_frac` 0.15 / 0.20). Two variants; 10% counts from
M72. Known before: only M72's stop10 numbers.

Also run now, before reading any candidate result, so the robustness view is not chosen after the fact: stops 10/15/20
at decision offsets 5/10/15, at 25 bps, and on 2007–09 at all four offsets.
