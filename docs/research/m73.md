# M73: stop-loss levels on the current pin

## Question

M72's 10% stop (Han, Zhou & Zhu 2016) cut the pin's max DD from 26.1% to 20.1% (Calmar 1.346) but failed folds 1 and 2:
it sells winners on ordinary dips in bull years. Does a looser stop keep the crash cut and lose less in those years?

Candidates: `m73_stop15_b2`, `m73_stop20_b2` (the pin with `stop_loss_frac` 0.15 / 0.20). Two variants; 10% counts from
M72. Known before: only M72's stop10 numbers.

Also run now, before reading any candidate result, so the robustness view is not chosen after the fact: stops 10/15/20
at decision offsets 5/10/15, at 25 bps, and on 2007–09 at all four offsets.

## Bar

Usual bar against the pin (1.125; 1.598 / 2.072 / 1.362) and baselines. A passer then needs: 3 of 4 offsets above the
pin's (1.125 / 1.101 / 1.055 / 1.142); 25 bps above 1.078 and its folds (1.498 / 1.971 / 1.310); 2007–09 four-offset
mean ≥ 0.284; and its four-tranche blend above the pin's (1.105; 1.590 / 2.199 / 1.312).

Bootstrap (`notebooks/m73_bootstrap.py`, M20 settings): the stop tranches have the higher Calmar in **86.9%** of
resamples against the pin tranches (M61: 59%), but the lower CAGR in 99.5% (5–95%: −4.35 to −0.94 points).

Where it comes from: March 2020 trough 17.8% vs 26.7%; Apr–Jun 2022 17.7% vs 22.8%; the 2010–11, 2015–16 and Nov 2016
drawdowns are about equal. The CAGR cost is mostly 2021 (+90% vs +117%) plus about 1 point every year.

So the 10% stop is the most robust drawdown cut found: every decision day, both cost levels and 2007–09. It costs about
2.5 points of CAGR and loses the bull-year folds by 0.06–0.10. It fails the strict bar, so the pin is unchanged.
