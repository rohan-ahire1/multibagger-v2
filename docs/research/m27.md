# M27: rotate between the stock book and gold by their recent returns

Pre-registration, written and committed before the code is used for any run (2026-10-01, Claude, at the user's
request for a Calmar near 1 and to use published research). Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no
`--final`.

## Why

M19 showed that gold, which does not fall with Indian stocks, lifts Calmar when held beside the momentum book. A fixed
20% share cannot respond: it drags in 2014–17 and is too small in bad years. Momentum across asset classes is among
the best documented effects (Faber 2007; Antonacci, *Dual Momentum*, 2014; Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012), and
Keller's asset-allocation models (PAA, VAA, DAA, BAA, 2016–2022) all choose between a risky and a safe universe by
recent returns over 1, 3, 6 and 12 months. Here the "assets" are the momentum book itself and GOLDBEES: each month the
book goes to whichever has done better recently.

M22 timed gold on its own trend and failed fold 2. This rule is different: it compares gold with the book, so it moves
out of stocks (not just into or out of gold), and it averages over four lookbacks instead of betting on one.

## The rule (`PortfolioM27.rotation`, a new config model `ParamsM27`)

1. A **shadow book** is the same strategy without rotation, run from the first session in the panel (2005) at full
   investment, with the market's costs. Its equity is the book's track record.
2. At each decision date and for each lookback L in `lookbacks_sessions`, every candidate (the shadow book and each
   symbol in `assets`) is scored by its return over the last L sessions (shadow equity, or the symbol's adjusted
   close, carried forward over missing days). A symbol without a close L sessions earlier is not a candidate for that
   L. The best `top_k` candidates get 1 / `top_k` each. With `cash_floor`, a winner whose return is not above 0 gets
   its share as cash (Antonacci's absolute momentum).
3. The shares are averaged over the lookbacks. The picks share the book's part in their usual relative weights
   (inverse-vol); each asset is held at its part; the rest is cash. The simulator trades the difference at the next
   open with the normal costs.

`fixed_weights` must be empty when `rotation` is set. Paper trading refuses `rotation` for now.

## Runs (question `momentum-m27-rotation`, against the pin `momentum/m19_gold20_invvol_b2`, Calmar 0.712)

All are `m16_invvol_b2` (the pin without its gold) with `fixed_weights: {}` and:

| Config | assets | lookbacks | top_k | cash_floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `m27_rot_gold_b2` | GOLDBEES | 21, 63, 126, 252 | 1 | false |
| `m27_rot_gold_cash_b2` | GOLDBEES | 21, 63, 126, 252 | 1 | true |
| `m27_rot_gold_mon_b2` | GOLDBEES, MON100 | 21, 63, 126, 252 | 2 | false |

The lookbacks are Keller's 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, taken from the papers and not tuned here. No other variants.

`m27_rot_gold_mon_b2` is **information only**. MON100 (Nasdaq-100 ETF) has traded 10–30% above its NAV since 2022,
because the RBI limit on overseas investment by funds stopped new units being created; its exchange price, used here,
includes that premium, which a buyer today would pay and could lose. It also starts on 2011-03-31. It cannot be
adopted for paper or live trading, whatever it shows.

Adoption as always: beat the pin and both baselines on Calmar, whole period and every fold. A passing config then
faces, in order, and is recommended only if it passes all of them:

1. **Offsets**: the M15 decision offsets 5, 10 and 15 sessions, against the M19 gold20 offset runs; it must win at
   least 3 of the 4 offsets (0 included) on whole-period Calmar.
2. **Costs**: 25 bps slippage a side (`nse_cash_slip25`), against `m20_gold20_slip25_b2`.
3. **Early window** 2007-04-02..2009-12-31 on `nse_research@2026-09-30d`, against `m26_gold20_invvol_b2` (Calmar
   0.209, max DD 48%): it must have the higher Calmar.
4. **Bootstrap** (the M17/M20 block bootstrap of daily returns): it must beat the pin in at least 80% of resamples.

## Disclosure

- Known before: all results up to M26, including that every momentum book lost 48–78% in 2008.
- Before writing this, an exploratory script (scratch, not committed) mixed the pin's stock-book equity curve with
  GOLDBEES and MON100 prices month by month, without costs. It showed: static mixes top out near Calmar 0.87
  (30% book / 40% gold / 30% MON100); rotating among book, gold and MON100 (top 2, weights averaged over lookbacks
  63/126/189/252) gave about 1.05 with a 21% max DD (March 2020); book-or-gold rotation over the same lookbacks gave
  0.93; ranking on Keller's 13612W score instead gave 0.89–0.91; the same rotation scored on one averaged return gave
  0.66–0.84. Single lookbacks ranged from 0.78 to 1.27. The results depend a lot on construction details, which is
  why the lookbacks here come from the papers and why the offset, cost, early-window and bootstrap checks are required.
- Scaling the book by its own volatility (Barroso & Santa-Clara 2015) was also tried in that script and did nothing
  for the March 2020 drawdown; it is not run here.
- Expected weak spots: whipsaws when stocks and gold trade places often (2013, 2016, 2021–22), and the cost of moving
  the whole book in and out of 40 stocks.
