# M13 research: a clean stocks-only universe, and how the inverse-vol result holds up to costs and size

Pre-registration, written and committed before any M13 run (2026-09-30, Claude working alone overnight at the user's
request). Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`. Dataset `nse_research@2026-09-29f`.

## Why

M5–M12 have tried 16 ideas on this period; one (M11 inverse-vol weights) passed. A 17th idea would add more luck than
signal. M13 tries no new idea: it checks the one that passed.

1. **Fund units leak before mid-2011** (`docs/research/m11.md`, "Checks after the verdict"): 32 ETFs have
   `fallback:` ids until their ISIN appears, so `exclude_fund_units` misses them. GOLDBEES is bought 1–2 times in 2011.
2. **Costs.** Inverse-vol puts more money in calm, often large names and less in volatile small ones. If its gain came
   from lower trading costs, or vanished when costs rise, it would not be worth moving the paper book for.
3. **Size.** The paper books hold ₹10 lakh. The weights only matter if they can be traded at a larger size too.

## New option (a `ParamsM13` model; older configs keep their model and hash)

- `universe.fund_units_by_symbol`: with `exclude_fund_units`, also drop every row of a symbol that has an `isin:INF`
  id on any row of the panel the strategy reads (the backtest panel is cut at `period.end`; paper trading reads its
  history window). This uses later rows to classify a symbol as a fund, not its prices: a symbol's type does not change.
  Paper trading accepts it (live ids are ISINs, so it changes nothing there).

Expected: no GOLDBEES (or any `INF` symbol) trade; each result within 0.02 Calmar of its M11/pin counterpart. If a gap

**Question `momentum-m13-costs`**: the same four configs on market `nse_cash_slip25` (identical to `nse_cash` except
slippage 25 bps a side instead of 10, i.e. 2.5×; charges unchanged). Pass: inverse-vol still beats equal weight on
whole-period Calmar on both books, and on at least two of three folds on each.

**Capacity** (no runs; `notebooks/m13_capacity.py`): for each buy and sell fill of `m13_clean_invvol_b2` and
`m13_clean_b2`, fill value as a share of that symbol's raw ₹ turnover that session. Fills scale with capital, so report
the median, p95 and max share at ₹10 lakh, and the capital at which p95 reaches 5% of a day's turnover. Inverse-vol
passes if that capital is no lower than equal weight's.

## Decision rule

The M11 recommendation (book 2 with inverse-vol) stands if the clean runs match within 0.02 Calmar and the cost and
capacity checks pass. If either fails, the recommendation is withdrawn and the draft paper config
(`configs/paper/momentum_top40_invvol.yaml`) is marked not to deploy. A clean paper draft pointing at
`m13_clean_invvol_b2` replaces the current draft only if everything passes; nothing is deployed without the user.

## Disclosure

- The fix's effect is known in outline (the 2011 GOLDBEES trades); nothing else about these runs is known.
- The slippage level (25 bps) was picked before running as a plausible upper bound for ₹5 crore-ADV names at
  small size, not tuned.

split such an order over days.

**Found on the way: renamed stocks lose their history.** 20 of `m13_clean_invvol_b2`'s sells (16 in book 1) had no bar
on the day, and the engine sold at the last close. Most were renames: the old symbol's bars stop and the new symbol
starts with no history, so the stock cannot be ranked for a year and a held one is sold at a stale price. FORCEMOT is
absent from NSE's own bhavcopies from about 2023-10-31 to 2024-02-14 (a source gap). Fixed at dataset level in M14
(`docs/research/m14.md`). Live note: the paper books (and M7 live trading) would also lose a held name at a rename.

Decision: all checks pass, so the M11 recommendation (book 2 with inverse-vol weights) stands. Nothing is deployed; the
paper draft is for the user to approve.
