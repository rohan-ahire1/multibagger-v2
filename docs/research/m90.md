# M90: how robust is M89? (information only)

M89's books (no fixed gold, 10% stop, 200-day NIFTYBEES filter with the risk-off share in gold) are the strongest
found but fail fold 1. Before anyone relies on them, three checks, none of which can adopt anything:

1. **Neighbours.** The 200 sessions and the 0 / 0.5 risk-off shares were not tuned, but nearby values should not
   collapse. Configs: `m90_r0g_sma150`, `m90_r0g_sma250`, `m90_r50g_sma150`, `m90_r50g_sma250`, `m90_r25g` (risk-off
   share 0.25 at 200 sessions), each at offsets 0/5/10/15 and in tranches.
2. **Bootstrap** (M20 settings, `notebooks/m90_bootstrap.py`): r0g and r50g tranches vs the pin's tranches and vs
   M78 half_s10 tranches.
3. **Out of sample** (M52/M85 stitching, `notebooks/m90_stitched.py`): the same rule on M42's walk-forward settings A
   (2014–17), B (2018–20), C (2021–23), at offset 0 and in tranches; configs `m90_{a,c}_r0g*`, `m90_{a,c}_r50g*`
   (the B piece is M89's own runs).
