# M29: a low-volatility stock sleeve beside the momentum book

Pre-registration (2026-10-01, Claude), written and committed before the code is used for any run. Development period
2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`.

## Why

M27/M28 showed that timing moves between stocks and gold does not raise robust Calmar. The other well-documented way
to cut drawdowns is to hold a second return source that falls less in the same crashes. Of the classic factors, value
and quality need fundamentals this project does not have; low volatility needs only prices. Low-volatility stocks earn
about the market's return with clearly lower risk (Blitz & van Vliet 2007, "The Volatility Effect"; Baker, Bradley &
Wurgler 2011; Frazzini & Pedersen 2014), the effect is reported for India (Joshipura & Joshipura 2016, Indian
Journal of Finance; NSE's Nifty Low Volatility 30 index, which ranks by one-year daily volatility), and low volatility
and momentum are only partly correlated, so holding both should smooth the book (Asness, Moskowitz & Pedersen 2013
on combining factors).

## The rule (`RankingM29.order`, a new config model `ParamsM29`)

`order: low_vol` ranks the eligible names by the standard deviation of daily log returns over `lookback_sessions -
skip_sessions` sessions ending `skip_sessions` before the decision date, lowest first (ties by symbol). `order: score`
is the usual ranking. `low_vol` needs `score: return_over_vol` (which computes that volatility) and no other ranking
option (second lookback, 52-week high, residual, frog, MAX filter).

## Runs (question `momentum-m29-lowvol`, against the pin `momentum/m19_gold20_invvol_b2`, Calmar 0.712)

| Config | What |
|---|---|
| `m29_lowvol_gold20_b2` | the pin with `order: low_vol`, `lookback_sessions: 252`, `skip_sessions: 0` (NSE's one-year window): the 40 least volatile liquid stocks, inverse-vol weights, 20% GOLDBEES |
| `blend/m29_mix_b2` | 50% the pin + 50% `m29_lowvol_gold20_b2`, never rebalanced against each other |

Everything else as in the pin (universe, top 40, hold buffer 40, resize, monthly at the month-end). No other variants.
The mix is the candidate; the low-vol book alone is reported for understanding and is a candidate only on its own
merits. Adoption as always: beat the pin and both baselines on Calmar, whole period and every fold.

A passing config must then pass, in order:

1. **Offsets** 5, 10, 15 (sleeves at those offsets, against the M19 gold20 offset runs): win at least 3 of 4.
2. **Costs**: 25 bps slippage, against `m20_gold20_slip25_b2`.
3. **Early window** 2007-04-02..2009-12-31 on `nse_research@2026-09-30d`: higher Calmar than `m26_gold20_invvol_b2`
   (0.209).
4. **Bootstrap** (M20 settings): beats the pin in at least 80% of resamples.

## Disclosure

- Known before: all results to M28. No low-volatility portfolio has been run or computed on this data before this
  note.
- Expected: the low-vol book alone has lower CAGR than the pin; the question is whether its drawdowns (March 2020,
  2018 small-cap fall) are small enough that the mix's Calmar rises. Low-vol in India is heavy in consumer staples,
  pharma and IT; the risk is a fold where those lag (2017 small-cap rally).
