# M18: demergers in the paper books' daily data

Data correction, written before the new daily datasets are built (2026-09-30, Claude working alone overnight at the
user's request). No backtest and no strategy change; the development period is untouched.

## The problem

The paper books rank on `nse_daily` (bars from 2023). A demerger the feed lists without a factor leaves the parent's
price series unadjusted, so on the ex-date the price drops by the value of the part spun off. For momentum this looks
like a crash: the name ranks low for a year and is never picked, and the drop is also an unexplained jump.

At the 2026-09-28 close (`nse_daily_linked@2026-09-28`), 8 names that pass the books' price and turnover filters
(₹5 crore ADV, price ₹50) have an unexplained jump since 2025-06. None has an override:

| Symbol (bars) | Ex-date | Jump | Feed | Cum close → ex-date open |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TATAMOTORS (now TMPV) | 2025-10-14 | ×0.598 | demerger | 660.75 → 400 |
| SKFINDIA | 2025-10-15 | ×0.452 | demerger | 5008.4 → 2300 (BE) |
| VEDL | 2026-04-30 | ×0.351 | demerger | 773.6 → 289.5 |
| TRIVENI | 2026-07-22 | ×0.584 | demerger | 471.5 → 289.95 (BE) |
| INDIAGLYCO | 2026-09-02 | ×0.212 | demerger | 1111.7 → 225 |
| HEG (now HEGAM) | 2026-09-07 | ×0.374 | demerger | 728.25 → 260 (BE) |
| SILVERADD | 2026-08-28 | ×0.102 | none | 228.41 → 23.97 |
| POLICYBZR | 2026-09-24 | ×0.640 | none | 1886.3 → 1697.7 open, 1207.2 close |

## The fix

Six manual `demerger` overrides in `configs/data/corporate_actions_overrides.csv`, one per feed demerger, with the
factor set the M16 way (ex-date open / cum close) and written against the bar symbol on the ex-date (TATAMOTORS and HEG;
the linked data moves them to TMPV and HEGAM with their bars).

Not changed:

- SILVERADD is a DSP silver ETF. On 2026-08-28 it moved to a new ISIN at about a tenth of the price with turnover
  unchanged, which looks like a 10:1 unit split, but NSE's feed has no record of it. The books are stocks only, so it
  cannot be picked; left for the user to confirm.
- POLICYBZR fell 36% on 27 million shares (ten times normal) with no corporate action: most likely a real move. It
  stays an unexplained jump until the user checks it and adds it to `verified_price_moves.csv` or not.

## Check

Build `nse_daily@2026-09-28b` and `nse_daily_linked@2026-09-28b` locally from the same raw files as the `2026-09-28`
versions. Report: whether the builds pass, the six jumps gone and nothing else changed, and the picks of the three
paper configs at the 2026-09-28 close on old and new data. Nothing on the VPS is touched; the books see the fix only
after the user merges and pulls.

## Results (2026-09-30)

Built at commit 157b4a7: `nse_daily@2026-09-28b` and `nse_daily_linked@2026-09-28b` both pass their checks.
Unexplained jumps fall from 144 to 134 in each, with no new ones. The ten removed are the six above plus four 2023
events fixed by M16's overrides (EDELWEISS, HERITGFOOD, MIRZAINT, NIITLTD), which the `2026-09-28` builds (commit
dbef58c) did not have yet. Only those ten symbols' prices change.

Picks at the 2026-08-31 and 2026-09-28 closes (the two month-end decision rules applied to data up to each date) are
the same on old and new data for all three paper configs (`momentum_voladj_stocks`, `momentum_top40_stocks`,
`momentum_top40_invvol`), and none is blocked by a jump. Rank among 1,071 eligible names for the inverse-vol draft at
2026-09-28, old → new: VEDL 717 → 83, TRIVENI 710 → 276, TMPV 965 → 944, SKFINDIA 918 → 994, INDIAGLYCO 168 → 169,
HEGAM 227 → 228. INDIAGLYCO's and HEG's drops fall inside the skipped last month of the lookback, so they start to
matter at the October month-end: on the old data both would then look like 60–80% crashes.

So the fix does not change the first decision at the 2026-09-30 close, but should reach the VPS before the October
month-end. That needs the user's merge and `git pull`; the daily job then builds a new dataset version by itself.
