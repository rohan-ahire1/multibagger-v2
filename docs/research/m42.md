# M42: walk-forward parameter search with Optuna (full run)

Pre-registration (2026-10-02, Claude), written and committed before any M42 search run. Follows the M41 pilot
(`docs/research/m41.md`). Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`. Dataset
`nse_research@2026-10-01c`. Base config and reference: the pin `momentum/m40_deliv40_gold20_b2`.

Planned for the VPS; at the user's later request it ran on the Mac instead (low priority, standalone process, not
inside a Claude session). Results are written to `reports/m42/summary.md` and copied into this file once read.

## Changes from the M41 pilot

- Wider ranges where the pilot hit the edge: GOLDBEES weight 0..0.6 (was 0.4) and `delivery_drop_frac` 0..0.8 (was 0.6).
- 300 trials per step (was 60), TPE seed 42, the pin queued as trial 0.
- A third step. Steps: **A** train 2010–2013 → test 2014–2017; **B** train 2010–2017 → test 2018–2023;
  **C** train 2010–2020 → test 2021–2023.
- Every reported setting is also scored at 25 bps slippage per side (M20 cost check), on the test fold and 2010–2023.

Everything else as in M41: the other five ranges, the objective (four-offset mean Calmar on the training window), the
data-gate rule, the selected setting (best training score), the top 5 shown as information, and the test-fold
comparison with the pin.

## What would count as a finding

A follow-up candidate (to be run through `mb backtest`, offsets, 25 bps, capacity, bootstrap and 2007–09 like any
other) needs, before it is even proposed:
- the selected setting of **every** step beats the pin's four-offset test-fold Calmar, and
- at 25 bps it still beats the pin at 25 bps on every test fold, and
- the selected settings of the three steps agree in direction (each setting moves the same way from the pin, or
  stays close to it).

If those fail, M42 is recorded as "tuning does not beat the pin out of sample" and the pin stays.
