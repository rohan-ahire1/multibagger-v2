## Why

The rotation in M59/M61 scores the book and gold on 1, 3, 6 and 12-month returns (Keller's 13612). Its result swings
with the decision day (M59 rotation alone: 0.75 to 1.24), and the 1-month return is the noisiest part. Faber (2007,
10-month average) and Antonacci (2014, 12-month) use only slow signals. Disclosure: M59/M61 were seen first; this is
one change to fix a weakness found there, and the offsets and 2007–09 are the test.
