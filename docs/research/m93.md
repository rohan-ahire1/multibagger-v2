# M93: M91's fixed gold, held only while gold trends up

## Question

M91 showed fixed gold trades fold 1 (2011, gold helps) against fold 2 (2014–17, gold costs return). M22's gold
trend rule (`fixed_trend_sessions: 252`, hold the fixed gold only if GOLDBEES is above its close 252 sessions earlier;
Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012) is on through 2010–12 and off for most of 2013–15. M22 failed fold 2 because the
switch left the 2015–16 stock drawdown unhedged; in the M89 family that drawdown is handled by the 200-day filter and
the stop, so the earlier failure need not repeat. M22 said no other lookback would be tried; this keeps 252.

| Candidate | Change |
|---|---|
| `m93_g25s10_r50g_gt_b2` | `m91_g25s10_r50g_b2` + `fixed_trend_sessions: 252` |
| `m93_g10s10_r50g_gt_b2` | `m91_g10s10_r50g_b2` + `fixed_trend_sessions: 252` |

Two variants, no tuning. Same checks and bar; tranches vs the pin's tranches. Disclosure: M22's on/off months and all
M88–M92 results were seen first.
