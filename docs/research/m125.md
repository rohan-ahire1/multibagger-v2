# M125: industry momentum as a buy filter on M106 (pre-registered 2026-10-23, before any M125 backtest)

Sixth cycle of the autonomous loop.

## Idea and sources

Moskowitz & Grinblatt (1999, *Journal of Finance*): much of individual-stock momentum is industry momentum; buying
winning industries and avoiding losing ones is profitable after size and value controls. In India, information
diffuses from the market to sectors gradually, making some sectors more profitable to hold than others (Pacific-Basin
Finance Journal 2014, "How profitable is the Indian stock market?", NSE). M106 ranks single stocks only; M72's sector
cap limited concentration but never used industry returns. Industry momentum was parked in cycle 2 for lack of an
industry map covering small caps. That is now built.

## Data

`mb data fetch industry configs/data/nse_industry.yaml` (new source `src/mb/sources/nse_industry.py`): NSE's
four-level classification from the quote API for the 2,611 symbols on NSE's equity list today. Snapshot
`data/raw/nse_industry/2026-10-22.parquet` (sha256 prefix 50e066c542ef5d4b): 2,595 rows, 2,366 classified (28 sectors, 65
industries, 194 basic industries); 229 rows unclassified (non-EQ series), 16 symbols failed (404).
Limits, stated: (1) not point-in-time: today's classes applied to 2010–23 (classes are mostly stable, but NSE moved to
this four-level scheme in 2023); (2) survivorship: delisted names have no class. They are unaffected by the rule and
are left out of every industry's mean, so weak industries' means are biased up (the delisted were mostly losers),
which weakens the rule rather than flattering it.

## Rule

At decision d, for each class at the chosen level with at least 3 of d's ranked (eligible) names: industry momentum =
equal-weighted mean of the members' adj_close 14 sessions ago / 273 sessions ago − 1 (M106's own window). The bottom
half of those classes (ceil, ties by name) are weak. A name not held is not bought if its class is weak (`nosell`,
M111's path); held names and unclassified names are untouched.
Code: `IndustryFilter`/`ParamsM125` (`params.py`), `weak_industry_names` (`targets.py`), `tests/test_m125_industry.py`.

## The bar (LOOP.md, unchanged)

Against `m120_ref*`: (1) Calmar higher before and after tax at offset 0, before tax at offsets 5/10/15; (2) no fold
lost; (3) higher at 25 bps; (4) neighbours (drop_frac 0.33/0.67; lookback 126 sessions as in Moskowitz & Grinblatt's
6 months) hold, bootstrap ≥ 85%. If both pass, the higher four-offset mean wins. No `--final` run.

Fail: any point above. Expected: weak to moderate. M106's 25 names are spread over many industries and their own
momentum already contains the industry's. Variants tried before M125: about 1,000; M125 adds 2.
