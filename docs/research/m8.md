## Question 1: `momentum-m8`

Pin to beat: `momentum/voladj_dev` (run 20260928-211436-f599c19f: 25.23% CAGR / 40.38% max DD / Calmar 0.62).

All runs use `nse_research@2026-09-29b` (the pin's dataset). The M8 options are new config keys, so the configs below
validate as the new `ParamsM8` model. `m8_control_dev` sets every option off; its metrics must equal the pin's exactly
before any variant is read.

Variants, each changing one thing from the control. This list is fixed; no other variants.

| Config | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `m8_top20_dev` | Hold 20 names | More concentration in the strongest names |
| `m8_top40_dev` | Hold 40 names | More diversification, smaller single-name hits |
| `m8_noresize_dev` | A name kept from last month is not traded; only exits and new names trade (each new name gets 1/30 of equity, scaled down to the cash from exits) | Lets winners run and cuts turnover |
| `m8_mix6_dev` | Order by the mean of two percentile ranks: vol-adjusted 12-1 and vol-adjusted 6-1 | Reacts faster to new leaders |
| `m8_sectorcap_dev` | At most 6 of the 30 names from one industry (`configs/reference/sector_map.csv`; unmapped names are not capped) | Less crowding into one theme |
| `m8_voltarget_dev` | Invest min(1, 20% / NIFTYBEES annualised volatility of daily log returns over 63 sessions) at each month-end; the rest is cash | Smaller book when the whole market is volatile (different from M5's trend filter, which failed) |

The values (20, 40, 126 sessions, 6 per industry, 20% over 63 sessions) are fixed here and not tuned.

Adoption: beat the pin and both baselines on Calmar, on the whole period and on each of the three folds. If more than one
variant passes, the one with the highest whole-period Calmar is the candidate. No combinations of variants are tried in
M8. A candidate gets one final run only on the user's say-so.
