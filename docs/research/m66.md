## Why

M42 offered ADV floors of ₹1, 2, 3 and 5 crore; steps B and C picked ₹1 crore, the lowest. As in M60 (delivery) and
M64 (top 15), this checks the far side of that edge. Thinner stocks carry a larger illiquidity premium (Amihud 2002)
but cost more to trade, so a gain on paper is not enough: the variant must also beat the pin after the M65 impact
charge at ₹10 lakh and ₹50 lakh.
