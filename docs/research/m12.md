# M12 research: drop lottery names, and go to cash when few names are rising

Pre-registration, written and committed before any M12 run (2026-09-30, Claude working alone overnight at the user's
request). Development period 2010-01-04..2023-12-31 only; no `--final`. Dataset `nse_research@2026-09-29f`. Adoption
metric: Calmar.

## Why

M11 found that the rebalance day alone moves the book's Calmar by up to 0.19, so from now on a pass also needs a
paired timing check (below). The two ideas here are filters, not new scores, and both target the drawdown:

| Idea | Source | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| **MAX filter** | Bali, Cakici & Whitelaw (2011), *Maxing Out: Stocks as Lotteries and the Cross-Section of Expected Returns*, J. Financial Economics | Stocks with the largest single-day return in the past month are overpriced by lottery demand and do worse next month |
| **Absolute momentum** | Antonacci (2013/2014), *Absolute Momentum: A Simple Rule-Based Strategy and Universal Trend-Following Overlay*; *Dual Momentum Investing* | Hold a relative-momentum winner only if its own past return is positive; otherwise hold cash. Cuts bear-market losses |

The M5 regime filter (whole book to cash below the index's 200-day average) failed. Absolute momentum is per name: the
book shrinks gradually as fewer names rise, and each empty slot is held as cash.

## New options (a `ParamsM12` model; older configs keep their model and hash)

- `ranking.max_filter_frac`, `ranking.max_window_sessions`: at each decision date, among the names that would be
  ranked, the `ceil(frac × n)` with the highest single-day simple return over the last `max_window_sessions` sessions
  (decision date included; ties by symbol) are removed from the ranking, so they are neither bought nor kept.
- `portfolio.min_momentum`: a name is ranked only if its 12-1 return (the same `lookback`/`skip` span, not vol-adjusted)
  is above this. Each slot is still 1 / `top_n` of equity: with fewer than `top_n` names left, the rest stays in cash.

Paper trading refuses both.

## Variants (four, no others)

| Config | Question | Change |
|---|---|---|
| `m12_max_b1` | `momentum-m12-b1` | Book 1 with `max_filter_frac` **0.10**, `max_window_sessions` **21** (the paper's top decile, one month) |
| `m12_absmom_b1` | `momentum-m12-b1` | Book 1 with `min_momentum` **0.0** |
| `m12_max_b2`, `m12_absmom_b2` | `momentum-m12-b2` | The same on book 2 |

Antonacci compares with the T-bill return; this data has no T-bill series and the market config earns no interest on
cash, so the hurdle is 0. References and pins as in M11: `momentum/voladj_stocks_dev_f` (book 1) and
`momentum/m8_top40_stocks_dev_f` (book 2). Control `m12_control_b1` (both options off) must equal book 1's pin exactly.

## Decision rule

An idea is **recommended** only if all hold:
1. It passes the usual bar (beat the pin and both baselines on Calmar, whole period and each fold) on both books.
2. Neighbours beat book 1's pin on whole-period Calmar (tag `momentum-m12-neighbours`): MAX frac 0.05 and 0.20;
   absolute-momentum hurdle −0.10 and +0.10.
3. Paired timing check (tag `momentum-m12-paired`): the idea at decision offsets 5, 10, 15 on both books beats the
   equal-weight M11 run at the same offset (`m11_offset*`) on whole-period Calmar at no fewer than five of the six.
4. The gain does not come from one fold alone.

Configs for 2 and 3 are written only for ideas that pass 1. No combinations with each other or with M11's inverse-vol
weights in M12.

## Disclosure

- Variants tried on this period before M12: 23 (M5 5, M8 6, M10 6, M11 6), plus two unread M10 dataset-e runs,
  neighbours and diagnostics. The paired timing check is there because of this count.
- The references' numbers are known; no M12 option has been run on this data before this note was committed.
- Bali, Cakici & Whitelaw sort all US stocks monthly; here the filter only removes names from a momentum list.
